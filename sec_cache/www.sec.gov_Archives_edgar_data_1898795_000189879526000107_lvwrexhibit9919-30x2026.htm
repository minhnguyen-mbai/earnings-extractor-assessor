LiveWire Group, Inc. Reports 2026 Third Quarter Financial Results

MILWAUKEE (October 29, 2026) – LiveWire Group, Inc. (“LiveWire” or the “Company”) (NYSE: LVWR) today reported third quarter 2026 results.

Third Quarter and Year-to-Date through September 30, 2026 Highlights and Financial Results

•Consolidated revenue increased 38% in the third quarter of 2026 compared to the same period prior year driven by increased unit sales in both the Electric Motorcycle and STACYC segments.
•Reduced net cash used by operating activities year-to-date through September 30, 2026 by 16%, driving a 16% improvement in year-to-date free cash flow as compared to the same period 2025.
•Year-to-date through September 30, 2026 market share of 80% in the U.S. electric motorcycle 50+ kilowatt on-road EV segment1.
•Delivered the first units of the S4 Honcho™ Trail to the U.S. market in August with the first units of S4 Honcho™ Street delivered to dealers in October. The first units of the S4 Honcho™ Trail are expected to be delivered to Europe in October with S4 Honcho™ Street expected to follow in November.

Liquidity and Going Concern
Management has determined that conditions and events raise substantial doubt about the Company's ability to continue as a going concern within one year after the date the Company's September 30, 2026 consolidated financial statements are issued. The Company's recurring operating losses, negative cash flows from operations, limited liquidity resources, and anticipated future funding requirements contributed to this determination. The Company continues to evaluate and pursue comprehensive financing and other strategic alternatives, which may include significant capital raises, strategic partnerships or investments, sales, and other strategic transactions, intended to support the Company's operations and execution of its business plan; however, there can be no assurance that such efforts will be successful or that any such transaction will be available on terms acceptable to the Company, our lienholders or stockholders.

In order to address these issues, and considering the debt maturity in December of 2027, on October 22, 2026, the board of directors of the Company (the “Board”) formed a special restructuring committee of the Board (the “Special Restructuring Committee”) and delegated to it the authority to (i) review, evaluate and negotiate, and, if deemed advisable by the Special Restructuring Committee, to recommend to the Board the approval or rejection of any debt or equity or other financing, sale, restructuring, recapitalization, strategic transaction or other similar opportunity or transaction for the Company (each, a “Strategic Transaction”), (ii) determine whether a Strategic Transaction involves a matter in which any director may have an interest that conflicts with, or is different from or in addition to, the interests of the Company and its stockholders generally (a “Potential Conflict Matter”), and (iii) create the officer position of Chief Restructuring Officer of the Company (“CRO”), to appoint such person to, and delegate such power and authority to, such officer position, and to remove and replace any person appointed to such officer position. The Special Restructuring Committee has sole and exclusive authority, on behalf of the Company, to approve or reject any Potential Conflict Matter. The Board may not

Mr. Gallagher will report to, and take direction from, the Special Restructuring Committee and be the principal executive officer of the Company.

Additional information regarding the Company's liquidity position, management's plans, and the going concern assessment will be included in the Company's Quarterly Report on Form 10-Q for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, which is expected to be filed on or before November 9, 2026.

Financial guidance

As a result of the actions discussed above, including the formation of the Special Restructuring Committee to review, evaluate and negotiate any debt or equity or other financing, sale, restructuring, recapitalization, strategic transaction or other transaction, we are withdrawing our previously issued guidance of a LiveWire Group operating loss between $70 and $80 million for the full year 2026.

About LiveWire
LiveWire has a dedicated focus on the electric motorcycle sector. LiveWire’s majority shareholder is Harley-Davidson, Inc. LiveWire comes from the lineage of Harley-Davidson and is capitalizing on a decade of its learnings in the EV sector. With a dedicated focus on EV, LiveWire plans to develop the technology of the future and to invest in the capabilities needed to lead the transformation of motorcycling. www.livewire.com
