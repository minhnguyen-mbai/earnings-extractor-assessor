LifeMD Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results

 

●Third quarter 2026 revenue of $44.6 million; adjusted EBITDA loss of approximately $0.4 million, within the Company’s guidance range of negative $1 million to positive $2 million.
●Adjusted EBITDA improved approximately $3.1 million sequentially from an adjusted EBITDA loss of $3.5 million in the second quarter of 2026, driven primarily by a 34% sequential reduction in selling and marketing expenses and reduction of overall non-clinical expenses.
●GAAP net loss from continuing operations attributable to common stockholders of $5.2 million, or $0.11 per share, compared with $8.4 million, or $0.18 per share, in the second quarter of 2026.
●Total active subscribers increased 13% year-over-year to approximately 350,000.
●LifeMD Pharmacy shipment volume grew 58.3% year-over-year.
●Exited the quarter with $21.9 million of cash and no debt; subsequent to quarter-end, closed on a $12.5 million senior secured term loan to replace the Company’s previous credit facility.
●Entered into arrangements with both Novo Nordisk and Eli Lilly, enabling LifeMD’s pharmacy to begin dispensing self-pay branded GLP-1 medications, including Wegovy, Wegovy Pill, Ozempic, Zepbound, and Foundayo, to patients nationwide.
●Entered into an exclusive relationship with AT&T to provide its 100 million wireless and fiber customers and 110,000 U.S. employees access to LifeMD’s healthcare and pharmacy services.
●Expecting fourth quarter 2026 revenue of between $46 million and $49 million, and Adjusted EBITDA of between negative $4 million to negative $2 million.

 

Conference call begins at 4:30 p.m. Eastern time today

 

NEW YORK, November 12, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) — LifeMD, Inc. (Nasdaq: LFMD), a leading provider of virtual primary care and pharmacy services, today reported financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

 

Management Commentary

 

“We ended the quarter with approximately 350,000 active subscribers, up 13% from a year ago and nearly flat from the second quarter, despite spending $9.5 million less on marketing,” said Justin Schreiber, Chairman and CEO of LifeMD. “We’re helping more people access high-quality healthcare, and we’re getting more efficient at doing it. From May to September, cost per acquisition fell 32% while monthly media spend declined 45%. Adjusted EBITDA improved approximately $3.1 million sequentially to roughly breakeven, and we expect to sustain or improve these unit economics going forward.

 

“Revenue of $44.6 million was below our guidance, and we are resetting our outlook for the fourth quarter and full year. We deliberately reduced marketing spend and prioritized longer patient commitments, and our partnerships took longer to ramp than we expected. Those factors weighed on revenue this quarter, but we believe our decisions put us in a stronger position to grow profitably.

 

 

 

 

“Our pharmacy has begun dispensing branded GLP-1 medications from Eli Lilly and Novo Nordisk. Our exclusive relationship with AT&T gives its 100 million wireless and fiber customers and 110,000 U.S. employees access to our healthcare and pharmacy services. We also made significant progress on our enterprise healthcare offering, which we will begin marketing to businesses across the country in the fourth quarter. Our women’s health program continued to improve, and we added experienced leadership to accelerate its growth. Our focus now is turning this work into revenue growth while maintaining the operating discipline we established this quarter.”

 

“Gross margin was approximately 82% in the quarter, compared with approximately 89% in the second quarter and 85% a year ago. The decline primarily reflects a change in presentation, not economics: under our new branded fulfillment arrangements, medications fulfilled by our own pharmacy are recognized on a gross revenue basis,” said Atul Kavthekar, Chief Financial Officer of LifeMD. “We reduced selling and marketing expense by $9.5 million sequentially to $18.5 million. General and administrative expense increased slightly from the second quarter to $14.7 million, reflecting clinical capacity added to support our new partnerships, partially offset by efficiencies in non-clinical functions. We ended the quarter with $21.9 million of cash and no debt. Subsequent to quarter end, we closed a $12.5 million non-convertible senior secured term loan with Avenue Capital. It replaces our prior revolving credit facility and gives us significantly greater flexibility to fund the working capital our branded fulfillment arrangements may require.”

 

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Highlights

 

All comparisons are with the third quarter of 2025 on a continuing operations basis (excluding WorkSimpli, which was divested on November 4, 2025, and is reported as discontinued operations for all periods presented). Non-GAAP financial measures referenced below are defined and reconciled to the most directly comparable GAAP measures at the end of this press release.

 

●Total revenue was $44.6 million, compared with $47.7 million in the prior-year period, a decrease of approximately 7%.
●Gross profit was $36.4 million, compared with $40.7 million in the prior-year period. Gross margin was approximately 82%, compared with approximately 85% in the prior-year period, a decline of approximately 370 basis points, primarily reflecting the gross revenue presentation of branded medications fulfilled by our pharmacy.
●Selling and marketing expenses decreased 18% year-over-year, and 34% sequentially, to $18.5 million.
●General and administrative expenses increased 6% year-over-year to $14.7 million, reflecting added clinical capacity.
●GAAP net loss from continuing operations attributable to common stockholders was $5.2 million, or $0.11 per share, compared with a GAAP net loss from continuing operations attributable to common stockholders of $5.4 million, or $0.12 per share, in the prior-year period.
●Adjusted EBITDA was a loss of approximately $0.4 million, compared with adjusted EBITDA of approximately $2.8 million in the prior-year period.
●Cash totaled $21.9 million as of September 30, 2026, and the Company had no debt at quarter end.
●The number of active subscribers increased 13% year-over-year to approximately 350,000 at quarter end.

About LifeMD, Inc.

 

LifeMD® is a leading virtual care company making high-quality healthcare more accessible, convenient, and affordable. Through its vertically integrated platform, LifeMD connects patients with a 50-state affiliated medical group, laboratory services, a state-of-the-art in-house pharmacy, and a U.S.-based patient care center. About the Use of Non-GAAP Financial Measures

 

To supplement our financial information presented in accordance with GAAP, we use adjusted EBITDA as a non-GAAP financial measure to clarify and enhance an understanding of past performance. We believe that the presentation of this financial measure enhances an investor’s understanding of our financial performance. We further believe that this financial measure is a useful financial metric to assess our operating performance from period-to-period by excluding certain items that we believe are not representative of our core business. We use certain financial measures for business planning purposes and in measuring our performance relative to that of our competitors.

 

Adjusted EBITDA is defined as net loss attributable to LifeMD, Inc. common stockholders before interest, taxes, depreciation, amortization, extraordinary litigation costs, severance and restructuring costs, acquisition expenses, insurance acceptance readiness expenses, preferred stock dividends, stock-based compensation expense, net income from discontinued operations and net income attributable to noncontrolling interests of discontinued operations. We have provided below a reconciliation of adjusted EBITDA to net loss attributable to LifeMD, Inc. common stockholders, its most directly comparable GAAP financial measure.

 

We believe the above financial measure is commonly used by investors to evaluate our performance and that of our competitors. However, our use of the term adjusted EBITDA may vary from that of others in our industry. Adjusted EBITDA should not be considered as an alternative to net loss before taxes, net loss per share, operating loss or any other performance measures derived in accordance with GAAP as measures of performance.
