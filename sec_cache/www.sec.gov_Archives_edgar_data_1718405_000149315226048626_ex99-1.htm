Hycroft Reports Q3 2026 Results and Maintains Strong Balance Sheet

 

October 27, 2026 – Winnemucca, NV – Hycroft Mining Holding Corporation (Nasdaq: HYMC) (“Hycroft” or “the Company”), a U.S.-based silver and gold development company that owns the Hycroft Mine, announces its third quarter results and the filing of its Quarterly Report on Form 10-Q.

 

Q3 Highlights

 

●Maintained safety record with more than 1.5 million work hours without a lost-time incident and a 0.86 total recordable injury frequency rate (“TRIFR”)
●Maintained strong balance sheet with $211 million in unrestricted cash and no debt, placing the Company in a strong financial position to support ongoing exploration, technical studies, and project advancement activities
●Continued the 2025-2026 Exploration Drill Program with approximately 18,700 meters drilled since the program began, with results continuing to demonstrate high-grade growth at Brimstone and Vortex
●Michael Deal Appointed Chief Operating Officer, bringing extensive mining, operational, and technical experience as the Company advances exploration, technical studies, and development planning
●Appointed four new independent directors, adding experience in mining operations, technical services, finance, and corporate governance to the Board of Directors

 

Current and Future Work

 

●The Company, in collaboration with RESPEC Company LLC and Hatch Ltd., has been focused on the conceptual design of a high-grade underground mine and processing facility. This work is substantially complete and will be incorporated in the project optimization work planned for 2027
●The current trade-off study between roasting and pressure oxidation (“POX”) processing methods is also being finalized in the fourth quarter with the results expected to be released in the first quarter of 2027
●Drilling at Brimstone and Vortex will continue as we grow and further define the full potential of the high-grade systems
●Heap leach metallurgical test work is underway and is expected to be completed in the first half of 2027

 

Diane Garrett, Executive Chairman and CEO, commented: “The third quarter marked meaningful progress for Hycroft, as we strengthened the organization and continued to expand the high-grade silver systems at Brimstone and Vortex. Both systems continue to grow and remain open in all directions and at depth. At the same time, we are optimizing development plans to unlock the highest value of this world-class resource for the benefit of shareholders and other stakeholders. Our expanded leadership team and four new independent directors bring the mining, technical, financial, and governance expertise needed to advance the project to production. With $211 million in cash and no debt, we are well funded to execute our plan.”

Organizational Update

 

Effective March 16, 2027, Stan Rideout, Chief Financial Officer, will transition to the newly created position of Executive Vice President, Strategic Advisor. Dr. Garrett commented, “Stan has been a valued partner whose financial leadership and strategic insight have been instrumental in strengthening Hycroft’s foundation and advancing our long-term objectives. I look forward to continuing to work with Stan.” The Company has commenced a search for a CFO.

 

Effective April 30, 2027, David Thomas, who previously served as Senior Vice President, General Manager and most recently, Senior Vice President, Corporate Affairs & Sustainability, will retire. To ensure a smooth transition, David will remain a consultant to the Company. Dr. Garrett commented, “Throughout his tenure, David has been a respected leader both within our organization and across the community where we operate. He played a pivotal role in strengthening our safety-first culture, reinforcing our commitment to ensuring every employee returns home safely each day. His leadership also helped foster a collaborative, values-driven corporate culture that has contributed significantly to our success. Beyond the Company, he built strong and lasting relationships with community leaders and stakeholders, establishing trust and support for our operations. I look forward to his continuing support as we advance the project.”

 

About Hycroft Mining Holding Corporation

 

Hycroft Mining Holding Corporation is a US-based gold and silver company exploring and developing the Hycroft Mine, among the world’s largest precious metals deposits, located in Nevada, a Tier-1 mining jurisdiction. With a history of heap leach operations, Hycroft is advancing to the next phase of operations for processing sulfide mineralization. In addition, Hycroft is engaged in a robust exploration drill program (2025-2026 Exploration Drill Program) to expand and advance the two new high-grade silver systems, Brimstone and Vortex. These discoveries represent a significant value driver for the Hycroft Mine.
