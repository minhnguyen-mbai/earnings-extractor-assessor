LENZ Therapeutics Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results, Announces Strategic Consumer Marketing Focused Reorganization and CEO Succession
Q3 2026 VIZZ® product sales increased 50% over prior quarter, with strong momentum continuing into Q4 as consumer awareness broadens and telehealth adoption increases
Strategic reorganization to prioritize consumer marketing, support the continued market expansion of VIZZ and reduce projected 2027 annual operating expenses by over 50%
Eef Schimmelpennink to transition from his role as President and Chief Executive Officer effective December 1, 2026; Dan Chevallard appointed Interim President and Chief Executive Officer
Management to host conference call on October 29, 2026, at 8:30 a.m. EDT
SAN DIEGO, CA – October 28, 2026 – LENZ Therapeutics, Inc. (Nasdaq: LENZ or “LENZ” or the “Company”), a pharmaceutical company focused on the commercialization of VIZZ® (aceclidine ophthalmic solution) 1.44%, the first and only aceclidine-based eye drop for the treatment of presbyopia, today reported financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, and announced a strategic reorganization to prioritize consumer marketing, allocate capital to maximize sales growth, and reduce operating expenses. The Company also announced that Eef Schimmelpennink will transition from his role as President and Chief Executive Officer effective December 1, 2026, with Dan Chevallard, its current Chief Financial Officer, to assume the role of Interim President and Chief Executive Officer.
“Our strong third-quarter results demonstrate continued progress in the commercialization of VIZZ, with product sales growth of 50%, increasing patient adoption and encouraging refill rates. We view these as promising indicators of our positive trajectory in this emerging market,” said Eef Schimmelpennink, President and Chief Executive Officer of LENZ Therapeutics. “We are focusing our commercial organization and investment on initiatives we believe will most effectively increase consumer awareness, access and adoption, while improving capital efficiency and supporting VIZZ’s continued growth. I am encouraged by the growing third-quarter consumer demand that is continuing into the fourth quarter, and excited about the opportunities ahead for LENZ.”
Mr. Schimmelpennink continued, “After nearly six years leading LENZ and more than 25 years in the biotechnology industry, I have decided to transition from my role as CEO effective December 1, 2026, to devote more time to other passions. I am very proud of the team we have built, the culture we have created and the successes we have achieved together. I am deeply grateful to our employees, the Board and our shareholders for their support. I believe as strongly as ever in LENZ’s future and have full confidence in Dan’s ability to lead the company. Dan and I will work closely to ensure a smooth handover, and I look forward to continuing to support him and the Board as a strategic advisor following the CEO transition.”
“We are deeply grateful to Eef for his leadership in building LENZ from our Series A into a well-capitalized public company and advancing VIZZ through clinical development, FDA approval and commercial launch. We wish him all the best for the future,” said Jeff George, Chairman of the Board of Directors of LENZ Therapeutics. “Dan brings a deep understanding of LENZ and our operating priorities and has our full confidence to lead the company as Interim CEO, with continued support from Eef and the Board through the transition. We are confident in the opportunity ahead for VIZZ and in our team’s ability to execute the more focused commercial strategy announced today.”
“I am grateful to Eef for his leadership and contributions to LENZ and look forward to building on the solid foundation we have established,” said Dan Chevallard, Chief Financial Officer and incoming Interim Chief Executive Officer of LENZ Therapeutics. “I am honored to lead LENZ through this next phase. Our priorities are clear: drive continued increases in consumer awareness, access and

    
adoption of VIZZ and deploy our resources with discipline. I firmly believe in VIZZ’s potential and our team’s ability to deliver on these priorities.”
Third Quarter 2026 and Recent Corporate Highlights
Commercial Update
•Q3 2026 product sales of $2.6 million on over 38,000 paid prescriptions, representing increases of approximately 50% and 51% over Q2 2026, respectively, with meaningful prescription growth observed across both the ePharmacy and retail distribution channels and momentum continuing into Q4.
•In July 2026, the Company launched a telehealth prescribing option to accelerate patient access to VIZZ, offering patients the convenience of an integrated online evaluation by independent licensed eye care providers, ePharmacy prescription fulfillment, and home delivery, in addition to existing access through eye care professionals. The telehealth channel contributed more than 50% of the incremental prescriptions in Q3 2026 over Q2 2026.
•Over 60% of ePharmacy patients have purchased more than one monthly pack of VIZZ since launch, confirming continued encouraging average refill rates from those starting VIZZ in Q4 2025 and Q1 2026 of five packs per year.
•Continued growth among prescribing eye care professionals (“ECPs”), with over 15,000 unique prescribers from launch through Q3 2026, with over 75% prescribing multiple times.
•In July 2026, the Company launched a nationwide TV advertising campaign and optimized its targeted digital consumer marketing, with an increased focus on those commercial activities most efficiently driving consumer awareness, adoption and new patient acquisition.
Strategic Reorganization
•Announced a strategic reorganization, implemented in Q4 2026, to streamline the organization and prioritize resources and capital on the initiatives expected to have the highest impact on sales. The reorganization is expected to enable LENZ to operate with a more focused and efficient organizational structure while retaining the capabilities necessary to support the continued commercialization of VIZZ.
•The Company expects the reorganization to reduce projected 2027 annualized operating expenses by more than 50%, targeting approximately $70 million to $75 million in 2027 operating expenses, excluding stock-based compensation.
•Substantially all activities related to the strategic reorganization are anticipated to be completed during Q4 2026 with an estimated $5 million in one-time net restructuring charges.
Leadership Transition
•Eef Schimmelpennink, President and Chief Executive Officer, will transition from his role effective December 1, 2026. Dan Chevallard, currently Chief Financial Officer, has been appointed Interim President and Chief Executive Officer, effective December 1, 2026. Mr. Schimmelpennink will lead the organization through the reorganization and support a seamless leadership transition by continuing to serve as a strategic advisor to the Company into 2027.

    
Global Regulatory and Commercialization Update
•In July 2026, under the exclusive distribution agreement with Lunatus for the Middle East region, the MAA was submitted under the Saudi Food and Drug Authority’s Abridged Pathway for the treatment of presbyopia in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. This submission represents the ninth ex-U.S. regulatory filing for VIZZ.
•In August and September 2026, under the licensing and commercialization agreement with Lotus Pharmaceutical, the MAA was submitted for the treatment of presbyopia in Malaysia and Vietnam, respectively. This represents the fifth and sixth submissions for approval under the agreement, and tenth and eleventh ex-U.S. regulatory filings for VIZZ, respectively.

About Presbyopia
Presbyopia is the inevitable loss of near vision associated with aging, impacting the daily lives of nearly all people over the age of 45. As people age, the crystalline lens in their eyes gradually hardens and becomes less able to change shape. This loss of elasticity reduces the lens’ ability to focus incoming light from near objects onto the retina. Adults over 50 years of age lose, on average, 1.5 lines of near vision every six years. Although the progression of presbyopia is gradual, presbyopes often experience an abrupt change in their daily life as the symptoms become more pronounced starting in their mid-40s, when reading glasses or other corrective aids are suddenly necessary to read text or conduct close-up work. Presbyopia is typically self-diagnosed and self-managed with over-the-counter reading glasses, or managed, after evaluation by an ECP, with prescription reading or bifocal glasses or multifocal contact lenses.
About VIZZ (aceclidine ophthalmic solution) 1.44%
VIZZ (aceclidine ophthalmic solution) 1.44% is a once-daily eye drop developed to restore clear near vision for up to 10 hours. Aceclidine is the sole active ingredient in VIZZ and provides rapid and durable near vision improvement. VIZZ is preservative-free and provided in single-dose vials. VIZZ is a predominantly pupil selective miotic that interacts with the iris with minimal ciliary muscle stimulation. VIZZ causes contraction of the iris sphincter muscle, resulting in a pinhole effect that extends depth of focus to improve vision. For more information, please visit www.VIZZ.com.
VIZZ Indication and Important Safety Information
INDICATION
VIZZ (aceclidine ophthalmic solution) 1.44% is a prescription eye drop used to treat age-related blurry near vision (presbyopia) in adults.
IMPORTANT SAFETY INFORMATION
•Do not use VIZZ if allergic to any of the ingredients.
•To help avoid potential eye injury or contamination of the product, do not allow the vial tip to touch the eye or any surfaces. Discard the opened vial immediately after use.
•Contact lenses should be removed before using VIZZ. After dosing, contact lenses can be reinserted after 10 minutes.
•If using more than one topical eye medication, the medicines should be administered at least 5 minutes apart.
•Temporary dim or dark vision may be experienced after using VIZZ. Do not drive or operate machinery if vision is not clear.
•Seek immediate medical care if sudden onset of flashing lights, floaters, or vision loss is experienced.

    
ADVERSE REACTIONS
The most common reported adverse reactions of participants were instillation site irritation (20%), dim vision (16%), and headache (13%). Adverse reactions reported in >5% of participants were conjunctival hyperemia (8%) and ocular hyperemia (7%). The majority of adverse reactions were mild, transient, and self-resolving.
