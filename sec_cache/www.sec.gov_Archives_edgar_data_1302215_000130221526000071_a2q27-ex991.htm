Houlihan Lokey Reports Second Quarter Fiscal 2027 Financial Results
– Second Quarter Fiscal 2027 Revenues of $692 million –
– Second Quarter Fiscal 2027 Diluted EPS of $1.64 –
– Adjusted Second Quarter Fiscal 2027 Diluted EPS of $1.85 –
– Announces Dividend of $0.70 per Share for Third Quarter Fiscal 2027 –

LOS ANGELES - October 28, 2026 - Houlihan Lokey, Inc. (NYSE:HLI) (“Houlihan Lokey” or the “Company”) today reported financial results for its second quarter ended September 30, 2026.
For the second quarter ended September 30, 2026, revenues were $692 million, compared with $660 million for the second quarter ended September 30, 2025.
Net income attributable to Houlihan Lokey, Inc. was $109 million, or $1.64 per diluted share, for the second quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared with $111 million, or $1.63 per diluted share, for the second quarter ended September 30, 2025. Adjusted net income attributable to Houlihan Lokey, Inc. was $124 million, or $1.85 per diluted share, for the second quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared with $127 million, or $1.84 per diluted share, for the second quarter ended September 30, 2025.
“Improving market conditions resulted in a stronger second quarter,” stated Scott Adelson, Chief Executive Officer of Houlihan Lokey. “While we remain cautious due to the volatility of external factors, we expect to see solid performance across our businesses for the remainder of the year.”

Revenues
For the second quarter ended September 30, 2026, revenues were $692 million, compared with $660 million for the second quarter ended September 30, 2025. For the second quarter ended September 30, 2026, Corporate Finance (“CF”) revenues increased 10%, Financial Restructuring (“FR”) revenues decreased (12)%, and Financial and Valuation Advisory (“FVA”) revenues increased 7%, in each case, when compared with the second quarter ended September 30, 2025.

Compensation expenses were $441 million for the second quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared with $423 million for the second quarter ended September 30, 2025. This resulted in a compensation ratio of 63.7% for the second quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared with 64.2% for the second quarter ended September 30, 2025. Adjusted compensation expenses were $426 million for the second quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared with $406 million for the second quarter ended September 30, 2025. This resulted in an adjusted compensation ratio of 61.5% for both the second quarter ended September 30, 2026 and the second quarter ended September 30, 2025. The increase in GAAP and adjusted compensation expenses was primarily a result of an increase in revenues for the quarter when compared with the same quarter last year.
Non-compensation expenses were $97 million for the second quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared with $86 million for the second quarter ended September 30, 2025. Adjusted non-compensation expenses were $91 million for the second quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared with $83 million for the second quarter ended September 30, 2025. The increase in GAAP and adjusted non-compensation expenses was primarily a result of an increase in rent expense and other operating expenses when compared with the same quarter last year.
The effective tax rate was 30.6% for the second quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared with 30.2% for the second quarter ended September 30, 2025. The adjusted effective tax rate was 30.0% for the second quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared with 29.7% for the second quarter ended September 30, 2025.

Segment Reporting for the Second Fiscal Quarter
Corporate Finance
CF revenues were $481 million for the second quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared with $439 million for the second quarter ended September 30, 2025. Revenues increased due to an increase in the average transaction fee on closed transactions, which was driven by transaction mix and improved market conditions for M&A and capital markets transactions. This increase was partially offset by a decline in the number of closed transactions, which we believe does not represent a trend.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures
As a supplement to our financial measures presented in accordance with U.S. Generally Accepted Accounting Principles (“GAAP”), the Company presents certain adjusted (non-GAAP) measures of financial performance. These non-GAAP financial measures are not intended to be considered in isolation from, as a substitute for, or as more important than, the financial information prepared and presented in accordance with GAAP. In addition, these non-GAAP measures have limitations in that they do not reflect all of the items associated with the Company’s results of operations as determined in accordance with GAAP.
Adjusted net income, total and on a per share basis, and certain adjusted items used to determine adjusted net income, are presented and discussed in this earnings press release and are non-GAAP measures that management believes, when presented together with comparable GAAP measures, are useful to investors in understanding the Company’s financial and operating performance. The non-GAAP financial measures exclude the following items, as applicable in any given reporting period:
•certain acquisition related costs, including (1) acquisition related deferred retention payments, which may be settled in cash or common stock of the Company; (2) amortization of intangible assets recognized in purchase accounting; (3) fair value remeasurements of acquisition-related contingent consideration; and (4) other integration and acquisition related costs, including asset write offs or impairments;
•legal and other professional fees associated with the simplification of our legal entity structure that has resulted from acquisitions;
•the income tax adjustments associated with the non-tax adjustments above, determined by applying the tax rates in effect in the jurisdictions for which the non-tax adjustment relates; and
•significant discrete tax related items, including (1) acquisition-related costs which are non-deductible for income tax purposes and (2) other unusual or unique tax-related items and activities.
In the future, the Company may also consider whether other items should also be excluded in calculating the non-GAAP financial measures used by the Company.

About Houlihan Lokey
Houlihan Lokey, Inc. (NYSE:HLI) is a leading global investment bank recognized for delivering independent strategic and financial advice to corporations, financial sponsors, and governments. With uniquely deep industry expertise, broad international reach, and a partnership approach rooted in trust, the firm provides innovative, integrated solutions across mergers and acquisitions, capital solutions, financial restructuring, and financial and valuation advisory. Our unmatched transaction volumes provide differentiated, data-driven perspectives that help our clients achieve their most critical goals. To learn more about Houlihan Lokey, please visit HL.com.
