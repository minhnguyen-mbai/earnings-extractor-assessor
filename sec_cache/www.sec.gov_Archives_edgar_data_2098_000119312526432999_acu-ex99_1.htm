ACME UNITED REPORTS THIRD QUARTER 2026 NET SALES INCREASE OF 18%

 

SHELTON, CT – October 27, 2026 – Acme United Corporation (NYSE American: ACU) today announced that net sales for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, were $58.0 million compared to $49.1 million for the same period in 2025, an increase of 18%. Excluding sales resulting from the acquisition of the assets of My Medic on January 15, 2026, comparable three-month sales increased 10%. Net sales for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, were $173.0 million, compared to $149.0 million in the same period in 2025, an increase of 16%. Excluding My Medic sales, comparable nine-month sales increased 8%.

 

Net income was $3.4 million, or $0.80 per diluted share, for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared to $1.9 million, or $0.46 per diluted share, for the same period in 2025, an increase of 80% in net income and 74% in diluted earnings per share. Net income for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, was $9.5 million, or $2.29 per diluted share, compared to $8.3 million, or $2.03 per diluted share, for the same period in 2025, an increase of 14% in net income and 13% in diluted earnings per share.

 

Chairman and CEO, Walter C. Johnsen said, “We had record sales in the quarter, with growth in all our business units and internationally. Back-to-school sales were strong. The My Medic integration is progressing on plan, and we are beginning to receive retail and distribution commitments for its product lines next year.”

 

The Company applied for and received $6.0 million in IEEPA tariff refunds and in the third quarter of 2026, the Company recognized $4.0 million of the refund as a reduction of cost of goods sold. The remaining $2.0 million was recorded as an accrued liability on the balance sheet as of September 30, 2026. In the third quarter, the Company also recorded $1.0 million of variable compensation expense related to the refund, included in selling,

Additionally, in the third quarter of 2026, cost of goods sold included $1.8 million of costs accrued in connection with the close of our DMT sharpening facility and the related move of production of the DMT products to more cost-effective facilities in Asia. Net income for the three- and nine-month periods ended September 30, 2026 also included approximately $0.4 million of tax benefits resulting from stock option exercises.

 

The My Medic business acquired in January 2026, sells tactical, trauma and emergency response products directly to consumers, contributed to sales growth but due to the seasonal nature of the My Medic business there was minimal impact on earnings in the third quarter and the first nine months of 2026. As a direct-to-consumer business, My Medic has historically generated the majority of its profitability in the fourth quarter and we expect this pattern to continue.

 

Mr. Johnsen continued, “Transportation and many raw materials are increasing in cost. We continue to address these issues by using new robotics in our plants, sourcing very competitively, and consolidating operations. Later this year, we plan to close our DMT plant in Massachusetts and shift production and fulfillment to other locations and offshore. The annual savings from the closure are expected to be more than $1.0 million in 2027.

 

Mr. Johnsen concluded, “Our balance sheet is strong, and we continue to look for acquisitions that fit our strategic criteria.”

 

For the third quarter of 2026, net sales in the U.S. segment increased 20% compared to the same period in 2025. For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, net sales in the U.S. segment increased 17% compared to the same period in 2025. The sales increases for the three- and nine-month periods were due to strong sales across all product lines and contribution from the acquisition of the My Medic business.

 

European net sales for the third quarter of 2026 increased 5% in U.S. dollars and 7% in local currency compared to the third quarter of 2025. Net sales for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, increased 20% in U.S. dollars and 15% in local currency compared to

Net sales in Canada for the third quarter of 2026 increased 9% in U.S. dollars and 12% in local currency compared to the same period in 2025. Net sales for the nine-month periods ended September 30, 2026, increased 8% in both U.S. dollars and local currency compared to the same period of 2025. The sales increases for the three- and nine-month periods were due to higher sales of first-aid products.

 

Gross margin was 45.3% in the third quarter of 2026 versus 39.1% in the comparable period last year. Gross margin for the third quarter included a benefit of approximately 690 basis points from the tariff refund partially offset by approximately 310 basis points related to the DMT facility closure costs. Gross margin was 42.6% for the nine-month period ended September 30, 2026, compared to 39.8% for the same period in 2025. Gross margin for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 included a benefit of approximately 230 basis points from the tariff refund partially offset by approximately 105 basis points related to the DMT closure costs. Excluding the impact of the tariff refund and DMT closure costs, gross margin remained above prior-year levels primarily driven by the inclusion of the new My Medic direct to consumer business.

 

The Company’s bank debt less cash as of September 30, 2026 was $29.7 million compared to $23.1 million as of September 30, 2025. During the twelve-month period ended September 30, 2026, the Company paid approximately $14.5 million for the acquisition of the assets of My Medic ($18.6 million purchase price less $4.1 million of holdbacks), distributed approximately $2.4 million in dividends on its common stock and purchased the cutting and sharpening line of products in Germany for approximately $1.6 million. During the same period, the Company generated approximately $13.8 million in free cash flow, including $6.0 million in tariff refunds.
