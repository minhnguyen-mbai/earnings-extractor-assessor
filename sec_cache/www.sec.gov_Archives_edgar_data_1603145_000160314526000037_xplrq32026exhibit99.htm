XPLR Infrastructure, LP reports third-quarter 2026 financial results
•Delivered solid third-quarter results
•Completed approximately 85% of planned 2026 repowerings to date
•Completed sales of approximately 400 megawatts of additional interconnection assets and rights to NextEra Energy Resources
•Updates financial outlook for adjusted EBITDA and free cash flow before growth

JUNO BEACH, Fla. - XPLR Infrastructure, LP (NYSE: XIFR) today reported third-quarter 2026 net income attributable to XPLR Infrastructure of $51 million. XPLR Infrastructure also reported third-quarter 2026 adjusted EBITDA of $471 million, and free cash flow before growth (FCFBG) for the third quarter of 2026 was $164 million.

"We delivered solid financial results in the third quarter while continuing to make progress across our key priorities," said Alan Liu, president and chief executive officer of XPLR Infrastructure. "We have now completed approximately 85% of our planned 2026 repowerings. We also completed sales of approximately 400 megawatts of additional interconnection assets and rights to NextEra Energy Resources, demonstrating the optionality embedded within our portfolio and our ability to unlock incremental value and redeploy capital across the portfolio. In addition, we retired the remaining balance of our 2026 unsecured notes. Today, with greater clarity on our full-year outlook as we approach year-end, we are providing an updated view of our 2026 financial outlook within our existing guidance ranges. In addition, we are introducing our 2027 financial expectations. We believe the underlying characteristics of the business support a durable profile over the longer term, with 2028 FCFBG expected to be in line with 2027. Looking ahead, we remain focused on strong execution, maintaining financial flexibility through disciplined capital allocation and pursuing opportunities that can enhance the long-term value of the XPLR Infrastructure portfolio."

Financial expectations
For calendar year 2026, XPLR Infrastructure now expects full-year adjusted EBITDA to finish in the lower half of the prior guidance range of $1.75 billion to $1.95 billion and full-year FCFBG to come in at or near the low end of the prior guidance range of $600 million to $700 million.

XPLR Infrastructure is also introducing its financial expectations for 2027. For calendar year 2027, the partnership expects adjusted EBITDA of $1.56 billion to $1.70 billion and FCFBG of $525 million to $600 million. Based on the current outlook, XPLR Infrastructure expects FCFBG in 2028 to be in line with 2027.

XPLR Infrastructure, LP
XPLR Infrastructure, LP (NYSE: XIFR) is a limited partnership that has an ownership interest in a clean energy infrastructure portfolio with long-term, stable cash flows. XPLR Infrastructure is focused on
1

delivering long-term value to its common unitholders through disciplined capital allocation of the cash flows generated by its assets and is positioning itself to benefit from the expected growth in the U.S. power sector. Headquartered in Juno Beach, Florida, XPLR Infrastructure's portfolio of contracted clean energy assets is diversified across generation technologies, including wind, solar and battery storage projects in the U.S. For more information, please visit: www.XPLRInfrastructure.com.

XPLR Infrastructure's management uses adjusted EBITDA and FCFBG, which are non-GAAP financial measures, internally for financial planning, analysis of performance and reporting of results to the board of directors. XPLR Infrastructure also uses these measures when communicating its financial results and earnings outlook to analysts and investors. XPLR Infrastructure's management believes that adjusted EBITDA and FCFBG provide a meaningful representation of XPLR Infrastructure's cash available for capital allocation. The attachments to this news release include a reconciliation of historical adjusted EBITDA and FCFBG to net income (loss), which is the most directly comparable GAAP measure.

XPLR Infrastructure does not provide a quantitative reconciliation of forward-looking adjusted EBITDA to GAAP net income, the most directly comparable GAAP financial measure, because certain information needed to reconcile this measure is not available without unreasonable efforts due to the inherent difficulty in forecasting and quantifying this measure. These items include, but are not limited to, unrealized gains and losses related to derivative transactions, which could significantly impact GAAP net income. Adjusted EBITDA and FCFBG expectations and other forward-looking statements assume, among other things, normal weather and operating conditions; positive macroeconomic conditions in the U.S.; public policy support for wind, solar and storage development and construction; market demand and transmission expansion support for wind, solar and storage development; access to capital at reasonable cost and terms; no changes to governmental policies or incentives; completion of certain repowerings; the sale or transfer of the assets underlying XPLR Renewables III (CEPF 3); and the transactions contemplated through the sale and co-investment agreement with NextEra Energy Resources, LLC. Please see the accompanying cautionary statements for a list of the risk factors that may affect future results. Adjusted EBITDA and FCFBG do not represent substitutes for net income, as prepared in accordance with GAAP.
