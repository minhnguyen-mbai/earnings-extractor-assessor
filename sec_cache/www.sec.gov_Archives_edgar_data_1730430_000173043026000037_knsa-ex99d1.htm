Kiniksa Pharmaceuticals Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Recent Portfolio Execution

– ARCALYST® (rilonacept) Q3 2026 net product revenue of $286.0 million, representing ~58% year-over-year growth –

– ARCALYST 2026 expected net product revenue increased to $1.045 - $1.050 billion –

– KPL-387 pivotal Phase 3 data expected in 2H 2027 –

– Conference call and webcast scheduled for 8:30 am ET today –

​

London – October 27, 2026 – Kiniksa Pharmaceuticals International, plc (Nasdaq: KNSA) (Kiniksa), a biopharmaceutical company developing and commercializing novel therapies for diseases with unmet need, with a focus on cardiovascular indications, today reported third quarter 2026 financial results and recent portfolio execution.

“Kiniksa continues to build a leading IL-1α and IL-1β inhibition franchise, driven by the commercialization of ARCALYST in recurrent pericarditis. In the third quarter, strong commercial execution led to gains in patient and physician adoption, resulting in revenue of $286.0 million and positioning ARCALYST to achieve blockbuster status this year,” said Sanj K. Patel, Chairman & Chief Executive Officer of Kiniksa. “Our robust financial position supports continued investment in value creation through ARCALYST, KPL-387, and KPL-1161. We now expect 2026 ARCALYST net sales of between $1.045 and $1.050 billion, up from our previous range of between $980 and $995 million. Additionally, we continue to enroll and dose patients in PASTORALE, the pivotal Phase 3 trial of KPL-387 evaluating once monthly subcutaneous dosing. We expect data in the second half of 2027, with the goal of bringing this important treatment option to patients in the 2028/2029 timeframe. We remain on track to initiate a Phase 1 trial of KPL-1161 by the end of this year.”

​

Portfolio Execution

ARCALYST (IL-1α and IL-1β cytokine trap)

●ARCALYST net product revenue was $286.0 million for the third quarter of 2026.
●As of the end of the third quarter of 2026, approximately 23% of the 14,000 annual multiple-recurrence patients were actively on ARCALYST treatment.
●Since launch, more than 5,450 prescribers have written ARCALYST prescriptions for recurrent pericarditis.

1

●As of the end of the third quarter of 2026, average total duration of ARCALYST therapy in recurrent pericarditis was approximately 3 years, in line with the median duration of disease.

​

KPL-387 (monoclonal antibody IL-1 receptor antagonist)

●Kiniksa is enrolling and dosing patients in the Phase 3 pivotal trial of KPL-387 in recurrent pericarditis, PASTORALE, and expects data in the second half of 2027.
●Kiniksa is also conducting a supplemental Phase 2 Transition to KPL-387 Monotherapy Dosing and Administration Study evaluating the transition of patients from standard therapies to KPL-387 monotherapy.

​

KPL-1161 (Fc-modified monoclonal antibody IL-1 receptor antagonist)

●Kiniksa expects to initiate a Phase 1 first-in-human clinical trial of KPL-1161 with a target profile of quarterly subcutaneous (SC) dosing by the end of 2026.

​

Financial Results

●Total revenue for the third quarter of 2026 was $286.0 million, compared to $180.9 million for the third quarter of 2025.
●Total operating expenses for the third quarter of 2026 were $231.7 million, compared to $156.8 million for the third quarter of 2025, and comprised the following:
—Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) expenses of $27.8 million, compared to $20.3 million for the third quarter of 2025. The increase in COGS expenses was primarily due to costs associated with increased sales of ARCALYST.
—Collaboration expenses of $105.8 million, compared to $63.3 million for the third quarter of 2025. Collaboration expenses are driven primarily by ARCALYST collaboration profitability.
—Research and Development (R&D) expenses of $32.8 million, compared to $24.2 million for the third quarter of 2025. The increase in R&D expenses was primarily due to increased KPL-387 clinical activity.
—Selling, General, and Administrative (SG&A) expenses of $65.4 million, compared to $49.1 million for the third quarter of 2025. The increase in SG&A expenses was primarily due to investment associated with the commercialization of ARCALYST.
—Total operating expenses for the third quarter of 2026 included $13.1 million in non-cash, share-based compensation expense, compared to $10.1 million for the third quarter of 2025.
●Net income for the third quarter of 2026 was $47.3 million, compared to $18.4 million for the third quarter of 2025.
●As of September 30, 2026, Kiniksa had $568.8 million of cash, cash equivalents, and short-term investments and no debt.

●Kiniksa expects 2026 R&D expenses of between $160 million and $170 million and 2026 SG&A expenses of between $270 million and $280 million.
●Kiniksa expects its current operating plan to remain cash flow positive on an annual basis.

About Kiniksa

Kiniksa is a biopharmaceutical company dedicated to improving the lives of patients suffering from debilitating diseases by discovering, acquiring, developing, and commercializing novel therapies for diseases with unmet need, with a focus on cardiovascular indications. Kiniksa’s portfolio of assets is based on strong biologic rationale or validated mechanisms and offers the potential for differentiation. For more information, please visit www.kiniksa.com.

​

About ARCALYST

ARCALYST is a weekly, subcutaneously injected recombinant dimeric fusion protein that blocks interleukin-1 alpha (IL-1α) and interleukin-1 beta (IL-1β) signaling. ARCALYST was discovered by Regeneron Pharmaceuticals, Inc. (Regeneron) and is approved by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) for recurrent pericarditis, cryopyrin-associated periodic syndromes (CAPS), including Familial Cold Autoinflammatory Syndrome and Muckle-Wells Syndrome, and deficiency of IL-1 receptor antagonist (DIRA). The FDA granted Breakthrough Therapy designation to ARCALYST for the treatment of recurrent pericarditis in 2019 and Orphan Drug exclusivity to ARCALYST in 2021 for the treatment of recurrent pericarditis and reduction in risk of recurrence in adults and pediatric patients 12 years and older. The European Commission granted Orphan Drug Designation to ARCALYST for the treatment of idiopathic pericarditis in 2021.

​

IMPORTANT SAFETY INFORMATION ABOUT ARCALYST

●ARCALYST may affect your immune system and can lower the ability of your immune system to fight infections. Serious infections, including life-threatening infections and death, have happened in patients taking ARCALYST. If you have any signs of an infection, call your doctor right away. Treatment with ARCALYST should be stopped if you get a serious infection. You should not begin treatment with ARCALYST if you have an infection or have infections that keep coming back (chronic infection).

3

●While taking ARCALYST, do not take other medicines that block interleukin-1, such as Kineret® (anakinra), or medicines that block tumor necrosis factor, such as Enbrel® (etanercept), Humira® (adalimumab), or Remicade® (infliximab), as this may increase your risk of getting a serious infection.
●Talk with your doctor about your vaccine history. Ask your doctor whether you should receive any vaccines before you begin treatment with ARCALYST.
●Medicines that affect the immune system may increase the risk of getting cancer.
●Stop taking ARCALYST and call your doctor or get emergency care right away if you have any symptoms of an allergic reaction.
●Your doctor will do blood tests to check for changes in your blood cholesterol and triglycerides.
●Common side effects include injection-site reactions (which may include pain, redness, swelling, itching, bruising, lumps, inflammation, skin rash, blisters, warmth, and bleeding at the injection site), upper respiratory tract infections, joint and muscle aches, rash, ear infection, sore throat, and runny nose.

​

For more information about ARCALYST, talk to your doctor and see the Product Information.

​

About KPL-387

KPL-387 is an independently developed, investigational, fully human immunoglobulin G2 (IgG2) monoclonal antibody that binds human interleukin-1 receptor 1 (IL-1R1), inhibiting the signaling of the cytokines IL-1α and IL-1β. Kiniksa believes KPL-387 could expand the treatment options for recurrent pericarditis patients by potentially enabling dosing with a single monthly SC self-injection in a liquid formulation. In October 2025, the FDA granted Orphan Drug Designation to KPL-387 for the treatment of pericarditis.

​

About PASTORALE

PASTORALE is a double-blind, placebo controlled, randomized withdrawal (RW) study enrolling up to approximately 85 patients with recurrent pericarditis. In the first period, a single-blind run-in (RI), all participants will receive KPL-387 while conventional oral pericarditis medications are weaned and discontinued. Participants achieving Clinical Response in the RI period will then be randomized in a 1:1 ratio to receive either KPL-387 300 mg SC once-monthly or placebo in an event-driven, double-blind, RW period. The primary efficacy endpoint is time to first-adjudicated pericarditis recurrence during the RW period. Participants in the RW period may be eligible to enter a long-term extension.

​

About KPL-1161

KPL-1161 is an independently developed, investigational, Fc-modified IgG2 monoclonal antibody that binds IL-1R1, inhibiting the signaling of the cytokines IL-1α and IL-1β, with a target profile of quarterly SC dosing. Kiniksa is currently engaging in preclinical development activities for KPL-1161.
