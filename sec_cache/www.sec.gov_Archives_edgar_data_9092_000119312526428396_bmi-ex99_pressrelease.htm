BADGER METER REPORTS THIRD QUARTER 2026 FINANCIAL RESULTS

 

Milwaukee, WI, October 22, 2026 - Badger Meter, Inc. (NYSE: BMI) today reported results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

 

Third Quarter 2026 Highlights

•
Total sales of $235.9 million increased modestly from the prior year’s $235.7 million and improved 6% sequentially from the second quarter. Base1 sales were $230.3 million.
•
Operating earnings increased 4% year-over-year to $47.7 million, with operating profit margins of 20.2% versus 19.6% in the prior year comparable quarter. Base operating earnings of $48.0 million increased 4% year-over-year, with Base operating profit margins of 20.9%.
•
Diluted earnings per share (EPS) of $1.24, a 4% increase from $1.19 in the third quarter of 2025.
•
Increased annual dividend rate by 10% to $1.76 per share, representing the 34th consecutive year of dividend growth.

 

“We were pleased with our third quarter financial results delivering continued sequential sales and operating profit improvement” said Kenneth C. Bockhorst, Chairman, President and Chief Executive Officer. “Consistent with our expectations, third quarter sales reflected the ongoing ramp of awarded utility water projects as well as solid underlying demand trends. I want to thank our employees across the globe for their commitment, contributions and ongoing customer focus.”

 

Third Quarter 2026 Operating Results

 

Utility water sales were flat year-over-year, including $5.6 million from the UDlive acquisition. Excluding the acquisition, utility water sales increased 5% sequentially from the second quarter, yet declined 3% year-over-year, reflecting uneven AMI project pacing partially offset by higher software revenue.

Sales of flow instrumentation products increased 2% year-over-year, with strong demand across the water-focused end markets against a difficult prior year comparison.

 

Operating earnings improved 4% year-over-year to $47.7 million, representing a 60-basis point improvement in operating profit margins to 20.2%. Base operating earnings of $48.0 million increased 4% year-over-year, with Base operating profit margins of 20.9%.

Gross margins of 41.9% were 120 basis points higher than the prior year’s 40.7%. Margin improvement reflects higher sales and structural mix benefits, while UDlive gross margins include the dilutive impact of inventory fair-value step-up amortization.

 

Selling, Engineering and Administration (SEA) expenses of $51.1 million were $1.3 million higher year-over-year. The inclusion of UDlive results along with related intangible asset amortization combined added $2.3 million to year-over-year expenses. Base SEA expenses of $48.8 million declined $0.9 million or 2% year-over-year due to the benefit of appropriate spending controls and lower incentive compensation.

 

The effective tax rate for the third quarter of 2026 was 25.1%, compared to 26.1% in the prior year. As a result of the above, EPS of $1.24 increased 4% from the prior year’s $1.19.

 

Outlook

Bockhorst concluded, “As we enter the final quarter of 2026, we continue to anticipate both sequential and year-over-year Base revenue improvement, inclusive of the fewer operating days typical for the fourth fiscal quarter, and expect to sustain this growth momentum into 2027. We remain steadfastly focused on cost management actions to mitigate the uneven 2026 sales cadence, while effectively addressing ongoing material cost pressures related to broad electronics supply chain dynamics.”

 

We maintain our focus on long-term strategic priorities including advancing the multi-year opportunity funnel for both metering and beyond the meter applications. We also continue to enhance our BlueEdge® portfolio of solutions against the backdrop of a multi-decade digital transformation of the global water sector, as demonstrated by the recent launch of OneNetwork™, our real-time network monitoring and analysis software platform.

 

During the quarter we repurchased approximately $55 million of our outstanding shares and increased our annual dividend rate for the 34th consecutive year, demonstrating our commitment to returning cash to shareholders.

 

In closing, I remain excited about the value creation opportunities ahead of us as we further our vision to preserve and protect the world’s most precious resource.”
