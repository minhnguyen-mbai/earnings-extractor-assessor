ECOLAB DELIVERS STRONG AND ACCELERATING OPERATING PERFORMANCE
REPORTED DILUTED EPS $1.95; ADJUSTED DILUTED EPS $2.18, +5%

THIRD QUARTER HIGHLIGHTS
•Strong execution drove broad-based acceleration, with reported sales growth of 13% and organic sales growth accelerating to 7%. Volume growth improved to 2% despite ongoing Middle East disruptions, while pricing accelerated to 5%.
•Growth in Ecolab's core businesses strengthened, led by accelerating growth in Food & Beverage and Light Water. Heavy Water and Paper both returned to solid growth.
•Ecolab's growth engines accelerated, driven by 63% organic sales growth in Global High-Tech and 17% organic sales growth in Life Sciences. Ovivo Electronics and CoolIT also performed exceptionally well and, one year after close, are expected to contribute approximately 2 percentage points to Ecolab's annual organic sales growth.
•Reported operating income margin 17.3%. Organic operating income margin 19.6%, +70 bps. Reported gross margin 44.1%. As expected, adjusted gross margin declined 70 bps due to the impact of M&A; excluding this, organic gross margin was stable as accelerating pricing offset rising commodity costs.
•Reported OI +7%. Adjusted OI growth +14%; organic OI growth accelerated to +11%.
•Reported diluted EPS $1.95. Adjusted diluted EPS $2.18, +5%, including an approximate 10% headwind from CoolIT acquisition-related amortization and financing costs.

2026 OUTLOOK
•2026: Expect adjusted diluted EPS in the $8.10 to $8.20 range, +8% to 9%.
•4Q 2026: Expect adjusted diluted EPS in the $2.13 to $2.23 range, +2% to 7%.
•Outlook reflects strong underlying performance and short-term CoolIT acquisition costs.

CEO Comment
Christophe Beck, Ecolab’s chairman, president and chief executive officer, said, “Third quarter results marked an inflection point in Ecolab's performance. Exceptional execution drove accelerating sales growth, margin expansion and double-digit adjusted operating income growth. Volume growth accelerated to 2% despite ongoing disruptions in the Middle East. As expected, pricing strengthened to 5% as we leveraged total value delivered to mitigate higher commodity costs while improving customer performance. This disciplined execution allowed us to stabilize our organic gross margin.

“The breadth of our performance makes these results even more encouraging. Growth across our core businesses strengthened, led by accelerating growth in Food & Beverage and Light Water, alongside consistent growth in Institutional & Specialty despite softer market trends. Performance in Paper and Heavy Water also improved, with both businesses returning to solid growth. Across these core businesses, our One Ecolab growth initiative is helping deliver greater customer value and share gains for Ecolab.

“Our growth engines continued to rapidly accelerate, with very strong performance across all businesses. Global High-Tech, our largest growth engine, significantly accelerated, growing 63% organically. Our recent acquisitions in this business, Ovivo Electronics and CoolIT, are also performing exceptionally well. This year, we expect Ovivo Electronics to grow mid-teens and CoolIT to more than double, compared with pre-acquisition sales. Together, these acquisitions are expected to add two percentage points to Ecolab's annual organic sales growth one year after close with increasing margins. Our differentiated water circularity solutions for microelectronics and next-gen liquid cooling technologies for AI data centers position Ecolab extremely well to capitalize on significant long-term growth opportunities.

“We are entering 2027 with momentum and confidence in our ability to deliver another very strong year. We expect organic sales to grow at the upper end of our 5% to 7% target and Ecolab's adjusted operating income margin to reach 20%. Adjusted EPS growth is expected to accelerate in the second half of the year as we lap M&A transaction impacts and operating performance continues to accelerate. This provides a clear path to meet or exceed our long-term adjusted EPS growth target of 12% to 15% in 2027. We have never been better positioned to deliver exceptional value for customers and shareholders.”

Third Quarter 2026 Consolidated Results
Ecolab’s third quarter reported sales increased 13% and organic sales growth accelerated to 7%. Ecolab Digital sales increased 34% to $129 million, with strong growth across both enabling hardware subscriptions and software.

Third quarter 2026 reported operating income increased 7% including the impact of special gains and charges. Adjusted operating income increased 14% as strong sales growth and improved productivity more than offset growth-oriented investments in the business and higher commodity costs.

Reported other income in the third quarter of 2026 decreased $4 million. Reported net interest expense increased $70 million reflecting the impact of financing the Ovivo Electronics and CoolIT acquisitions.

The reported income tax rate for the third quarter of 2026 was 20.2% compared with the reported rate of 17.8% in the third quarter of 2025. Excluding special gains and charges and discrete tax items, the adjusted tax rate for the third quarter of 2026 was 20.4% compared with the adjusted tax rate of 20.0% in the third quarter of 2025.

Reported net income decreased 6% versus the prior year. Excluding the impact of special gains and charges and discrete tax items, adjusted net income increased 4% versus the prior year, including an approximate 10% headwind from CoolIT acquisition-related amortization and financing costs.

Reported diluted earnings per share decreased 5% versus the prior year. Adjusted diluted earnings per share increased 5% when compared against the third quarter of 2025, including an approximate 10% headwind from CoolIT acquisition-related amortization and financing costs.

As expected, currency translation was neutral to adjusted diluted earnings per share in the third quarter of 2026.

Business Outlook
2026
Long-term growth trends in water, hygiene, infection prevention, and digital technologies continue to fuel durable demand for Ecolab’s innovative technologies and services. Strong momentum in Ecolab’s growth engines, which include Global High-Tech, Life Sciences, Pest Elimination and Ecolab Digital, is expected to continue to strengthen Ecolab’s overall performance. Ecolab’s investments in these areas position the company well to capitalize on these attractive long-term high-growth, high-margin opportunities.

In the near-term, the global operating environment remains complex, including constantly evolving geopolitics and international trade policy, which are resulting in rising commodity costs and customer disruptions in the Middle East. Importantly, the company is very well positioned to quickly mitigate the impact of these challenges. Over the past few years, Ecolab’s team has demonstrated it can adjust quickly to deliver high performance in almost any environment, which is why even with these dynamic macroeconomic conditions, Ecolab’s confidence in its performance trajectory remains strong.

As a result, Ecolab expects full year 2026 adjusted diluted earnings per share to be in the $8.10 to $8.20 range, rising 8% to 9% versus last year. This range reflects strong underlying performance and an approximate 6 percentage point short-term headwind to adjusted earnings per share growth from CoolIT acquisition-related amortization and financing costs.

The company currently anticipates quantifiable special charges in 2026 to be approximately $0.83 per share, principally related to restructuring charges and acquisition and integration costs. Other than the special gains and charges noted above, other such amounts are not currently quantifiable.

2026 – Fourth Quarter
With continued strong pricing, further volume growth, and the acquisitions of Ovivo Electronics and CoolIT, Ecolab expects reported sales in the fourth quarter of 2026 to increase 12% to 14%. Organic sales growth in the fourth quarter of 2026 is expected to remain strong in the 6% to 7% range. The company anticipates adjusted operating income margin in the fourth quarter to be approximately 19% and an organic operating income margin to be approximately 20%.

As a result, Ecolab expects fourth quarter 2026 adjusted diluted earnings per share in the $2.13 to $2.23 range, rising 2% to 7% compared with adjusted diluted earnings per share of $2.08 a year ago. This range reflects strong underlying performance and an approximate 12 percentage point short-term headwind from CoolIT acquisition-related amortization and financing costs.

The company currently expects quantifiable special charges in the fourth quarter of 2026 to be approximately $0.26 per share, principally related to acquisition and integration costs and restructuring charges. Other than the special gains and charges noted above, other such amounts are not currently quantifiable.

About Ecolab
A trusted partner for millions of customers, Ecolab (NYSE:ECL) is a global leader in water, hygiene and infection prevention solutions and services that protect people and the resources vital to life. For more than a century, Ecolab has advanced innovation by integrating science-based solutions, data-driven insights, AI technology and world-class service. This unique combination enables Ecolab to partner with customers to define what best-in-class looks like and scale it across their operations, helping them achieve peak performance. Today, Ecolab delivers $16 billion in annual sales, employs 48,000 associates and serves customers in more than 170 countries and 40 industries. The company helps protect one-third of the world’s food production and a quarter of the power generated while delivering innovative solutions across food, hospitality, healthcare, data centers, microelectronics and life sciences. As the world’s water company, Ecolab plays an important role in AI growth by supporting the full water needs of advanced computing—from ultra‑pure water for chip manufacturing, to water solutions that support the power behind AI, to direct liquid cooling systems for high‑density computing that improves performance while reducing environmental impact through circular water use. In life sciences, Ecolab delivers end to end solutions that support the development and manufacturing of life-saving drugs, helping customers operate safely and consistently at scale while improving performance and reducing environmental impact. Through its comprehensive approach, Ecolab protects what’s vital, with a goal by 2030 to
