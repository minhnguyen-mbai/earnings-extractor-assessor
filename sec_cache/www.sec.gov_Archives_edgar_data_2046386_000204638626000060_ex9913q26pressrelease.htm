Medline Reports Third Quarter and
Nine Months 2026 Results

•Third quarter net sales of $7.9 billion, an increase of 10.4%
•Third quarter net income of $318 million, a decrease of 1.2%
•Third quarter Adjusted EBITDA1 of $1.0 billion, an increase of 11.8%

•Nine months net sales of $22.9 billion, an increase of 10.9%
•Nine months net income of $696 million, a decrease of 28.8%
•Nine months Adjusted EBITDA1 of $2.8 billion, an increase of 5.0%

•Raising full year 2026 Organic Sales2 guidance to 10.0% to 10.5% from 9.0% to 10.0% and reaffirming Adjusted EBITDA2 guidance of $3.3 billion to $3.4 billion
•Board of Directors authorized a $1 billion share repurchase program

NORTHFIELD, Ill., Nov. 4, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Medline Inc. (“Medline” or the “Company”) (Nasdaq: MDLN), the largest provider of medical-surgical (“med-surg”) products and supply chain solutions serving all points of care3, today reported its operating results for the three and nine months ended September 26, 2026.

“Medline delivered another strong quarter, with double-digit net sales growth, reflecting continued momentum across our business,” said Jim Boyle, chief executive officer of Medline. “Our commercial execution remains strong, including continued progress toward our annual total new customer signings4 goal. We are advancing key strategic priorities, including capacity expansion and new technology across our distribution network and progress with our global quality initiatives. We remain focused on serving our customers, advancing our strategic priorities and driving sustainable long-term growth.”

Third Quarter 2026 Results

Third quarter 2026 net sales increased 10.4% to $7.9 billion, compared to $7.1 billion in the third quarter 2025, with Organic Sales¹ increasing 10.5%. This was primarily driven by existing customer growth and implementation of new customer signings from 2025 and includes an $11 million increase related to an adjustment of customer repayment amounts.

Third quarter 2026 net income decreased 1.2% to $318 million, compared to $322 million in the third quarter 2025, primarily driven by increased inventory costs associated with inflation, higher operating expenses, and customer mix. This was partially offset by higher net sales and net IEEPA tariff refund benefits.








Third quarter 2026 Adjusted EBITDA¹ increased 11.8% to $960 million, compared to $859 million in the third quarter 2025, primarily driven by higher net sales and net IEEPA tariff refund benefits, partially offset by higher operating expenses and customer mix.

Third quarter 2026 diluted earnings per share and Adjusted Diluted EPS¹ were $0.22 and $0.44, respectively.

Nine Months 2026 Results

Nine months 2026 net sales increased 10.9% to $22.9 billion, compared to $20.6 billion in the 2025 period, with Organic Sales¹ increasing 10.7%. This was primarily driven by implementation of new customer signings from 2025 and existing customer growth. This includes a $78 million reduction related to the accrued customer repayments associated with IEEPA tariff refunds.

Nine months 2026 net income decreased 28.8% to $696 million, compared to $977 million in the first nine months of 2025. This was primarily driven by higher operating expenses, net losses of $316 million related to the fire at the Company’s distribution center in Tracy, California, higher cost of goods sold including the impact of tariffs, customer and segment mix, and increased inventory costs associated with inflation. This was partially offset by higher net sales and net IEEPA tariff refund benefits.

Nine months 2026 Adjusted EBITDA¹ increased 5.0% to $2.8 billion, compared to $2.7 billion in the nine months 2025, primarily driven by higher net sales and net IEEPA tariff refund benefits, partially offset by higher operating expenses, higher cost of goods sold including the impact of tariffs, and customer and segment mix.

Nine months 2026 diluted earnings per share and Adjusted Diluted EPS¹ were $0.45 and $1.27, respectively.

Net cash provided by operating activities for the first nine months of 2026 was $2.2 billion, driven by net income, net of non-cash items and inclusive of tariff refunds received, and favorable working capital.

Free Cash Flow¹ for the first nine months of 2026 was $1.8 billion, driven by net cash provided by operating activities, partially offset by $348 million of capital expenditures, primarily related to investments in automation across the distribution network, construction of new distribution centers and expansion of kitting manufacturing capacity.

Capital Allocation

In October, the Medline Board of Directors approved a share repurchase program authorizing the Company, including Medline Holdings, LP, to repurchase up to $1 billion of the Company’s outstanding shares of Class A common stock and/or units of Medline Holdings, LP, and the Company prepaid $750 million in aggregate principal amount of the 2033 Refinancing Term Loan Facility.

These actions reflect Medline’s strong cash generation and disciplined capital allocation approach, including investing in the business, continued debt reduction and opportunistic return of capital to shareholders.

2026 Guidance
The Company is raising its full year 2026 outlook for Organic Sales2 growth to 10.0% to 10.5%, compared to its previous outlook of 9.0% to 10.0%, and reaffirming its Adjusted EBITDA2 outlook of $3.3 billion to $3.4 billion. Consistent with prior guidance, the Adjusted EBITDA outlook does not reflect IEEPA tariff refunds, including the $324 million of net tariff benefit through the first nine months of 2026.











IEEPA Tariff Refunds and Related Customer Repayments
Following the U.S. Supreme Court’s February 2026 ruling that IEEPA tariffs were unauthorized, the Company recognized $81 million and $324 million of net IEEPA tariff refund benefits during the three and nine months ended September 26, 2026, respectively. These amounts reflect tariff refunds of $70 million and $402 million, respectively, recorded as a reduction of cost of goods sold. For the three month period, the net benefit also included an $11 million increase in net sales related to an adjustment of customer repayment amounts and a corresponding reduction in the related accrual. For the nine month period, the net benefit was partially offset by a $78 million reduction in net sales related to accrued customer repayments associated with IEEPA tariff refunds. These amounts were recorded entirely within the Medline Brand segment and were included in net income and Adjusted EBITDA1.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures
The non-GAAP financial measures provided in this press release should be viewed in addition to, and not as an alternative for, results prepared in accordance with accounting principles generally accepted in the United States of America (“GAAP”).

To supplement the financial information provided, the Company has presented Organic Sales, Adjusted EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA Margin, Adjusted Net Income, Adjusted Diluted EPS, Free Cash Flow, and Net Leverage, which are considered non-GAAP financial measures. The non-GAAP financial measures presented may differ from similarly titled non-GAAP financial measures presented by other companies, and other companies may not define these non-GAAP financial measures in the same way. These measures are not substitutes for their comparable GAAP financial measures, such as net income/(loss), net income margin, diluted earnings per share, net cash from operating activities, net sales, or other measures prescribed by GAAP, and there are limitations to using non-GAAP financial measures.

Management uses these non-GAAP financial measures to assist in comparing the Company’s performance on a consistent basis for purposes of business decision making by removing the impact of certain items that management believes do not directly reflect the Company’s ongoing operating performance. The Company believes Organic Sales, Adjusted EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA Margin, Adjusted Net Income, and Adjusted Diluted EPS provide important comparability of ongoing operating performance, allowing investors and management to assess the Company’s operating performance on a consistent basis. The Company believes Free Cash Flow and Net Leverage provide a measure of the Company’s core operating performance, the cash-generating capabilities of the Company’s business operations, and are factors used in determining the Company’s borrowing capacity and the amount of cash available for debt repayments, acquisitions, and other corporate purposes.

Management believes that presenting the Company’s non-GAAP financial measures is useful to investors because it (i) provides investors with meaningful supplemental information regarding financial performance by excluding certain items that we do not consider indicative of our ongoing operating performance, (ii) permits investors to view performance using the same tools that management uses to budget, make operating and strategic decisions, and evaluate historical performance, and (iii) otherwise provides supplemental information that may be useful to investors in evaluating the Company’s results. The Company believes that the presentation of these non-GAAP financial measures, when considered together with the corresponding GAAP financial measures and the reconciliations to those measures, provides investors with greater understanding of the factors and trends affecting the Company’s business than could be obtained absent these disclosures.

Definitions
Organic Sales is defined as net sales excluding, when they occur, the impact of acquisitions, divestitures, and changes in foreign exchange rates from the net sales changes. The changes in foreign currency exchange rates from the net sales changes are calculated by translating current period GAAP results at the prior period foreign currency exchange rates and comparing these amounts to the current period GAAP results at the current period foreign currency exchange rates.









Adjusted EBITDA is defined as net income (loss) adjusted for (i) interest expense, net, (ii) provision for income taxes, (iii) depreciation and amortization, (iv) inventory-related adjustments, (v) stock-based compensation, (vi) litigation (gains) charges, net, (vii) transaction-related costs, and (viii) other non-core (gains) charges. Adjusted EBITDA Margin is defined as Adjusted EBITDA divided by net sales.

Adjusted Net Income is defined as net income (loss) adjusted for (i) intangible asset amortization, (ii) inventory-related adjustments, (iii) stock-based compensation, (iv) litigation (gains) charges, net, (v) transaction-related costs, (vi) other non-core (gains) charges, and (vii) tax impacts related to non-GAAP adjustments, noncontrolling interests conversion, and retained tax receivable agreement (“TRA”) benefits. Adjusted Diluted EPS is defined as Adjusted Net Income divided by adjusted weighted-average number of shares of common stock, diluted. The adjusted weighted shares calculation assumes the impact of certain antidilutive securities that were excluded from the U.S. GAAP diluted earnings per share.

Free Cash Flow is defined as net cash provided by/(used for) operating activities less net capital expenditures. The use of this non-GAAP measure does not imply or represent the residual cash flow for discretionary expenditures since the Company has certain non-discretionary obligations such as debt service that are not deducted from the measure.

Net Leverage is defined as net debt (total debt less cash, cash equivalents and short-term investments) divided by Adjusted EBITDA.
