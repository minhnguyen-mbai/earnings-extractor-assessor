Nuwellis Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Business Update Highlighted by 430 Basis Point Gross Margin Expansion and 13% Reduction in Operating Expenses, Driving 24% Improvement in Operating Loss
 
Console unit sales increased 88% year-to-date versus prior-year period, expanding its installed base to drive future recurring circuit revenue
 
Advanced growth strategy with 510(k) submission for Aquadex label expansion and two product portfolio-expanding agreements leveraging existing sales force
 
Company to conduct conference call beginning at 9:00 a.m. Eastern time today
 
MINNEAPOLIS – November 12, 2026 – Nuwellis, Inc. (Nasdaq: NUWE), a medical technology company committed to delivering solutions for patients with cardiorenal conditions, today reported financial results for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026.
 
Third Quarter 2026 Financial Highlights
 
●
Operating loss for the third quarter of 2026 improved 24% year over year to $2.1 million, driven by 430 basis points of gross margin expansion and a 13% reduction in operating expenses, primarily reflecting outsourced manufacturing efficiencies and efforts to align expenses with the highest-priority commercial and strategic programs.
●
Net sales were $2.2 million in the third quarter of 2026, essentially unchanged versus the prior-year quarter, as the prior-year period benefited from fulfilling $0.4 million in accumulated backorders. Net sales increased 12% sequentially in the third quarter of 2026. For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, net sales increased 13% to $6.6 million compared with $5.8 million in the prior-year period, driven by growth in U.S. circuit and console sales.
●
Pediatrics remained a significant growth driver, with net sales increasing 20% in the third quarter of 2026 and 26% year-to-date, compared with the prior-year periods.
●
Sold 32 Aquadex consoles in the first nine months of 2026, an 88% increase compared with 17 consoles in the prior-year period. The expanded installed base provides a strong foundation for future recurring circuit revenue.
●
Third-quarter 2026 gross margin expanded to 69.5% from 65.2% in the prior-year quarter, reflecting higher average selling prices and continued benefits from the Company's contract manufacturing model. Year-to-date gross margin improved to 71.7%, compared with 59.4% in the prior-year period.
●
Strengthened the Company's balance sheet as of September 30, 2026, with $8.3 million in cash and cash equivalents.

Business Updates
 
●
Submitted a 510(k) premarket notification to the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) seeking to expand the indication of its Aquadex SmartFlow® System to include pediatric patients weighing five kilograms and above.
●
A multicenter study titled “Kidney Support in Infants and Children using Aquadex: Findings From the ULTRA-Peds Registry” was accepted for publication in Pediatric Nephrology.
●
Entered into a co-promotion and distribution agreement with Atrility Medical for its AtriAmp postoperative cardiac rhythm monitoring device, which leverages Nuwellis’ current pediatric and critical care sales force.
●
Secured a second strategic distribution agreement with the exclusive U.S. distribution rights for the pediatric Zorro-Flow™ external urine collection system.
 
"We have made meaningful progress in strengthening Nuwellis both commercially and financially. Year to date, console unit sales have nearly doubled, expanding our installed base and creating a larger opportunity to grow recurring circuit revenue. Although our third quarter faced a tough comparison against the prior-year period when we fulfilled a significant backorder, the underlying momentum in our business remains strong, as evidenced by the growth in our pediatric segment," said Mike McCormick, Nuwellis President and Chief Executive. "Further, by expanding gross margins and maintaining disciplined cost management, we significantly narrowed our operating loss and cash use during the quarter while continuing to invest in the areas that matter most to our long-term growth.
 
"We also delivered on several key strategic initiatives, including the submission of a 510(k) to the FDA to expand the Aquadex label to pediatric patients weighing as little as five kilograms, which, if cleared, could expand access and increase adoption. In addition, we secured distribution agreements with Atrility Medical and Zorro-Flow, which broaden our product offering in a capital-efficient manner through our established commercial infrastructure. We believe these achievements position Nuwellis for sustainable growth and long-term value creation for patients, customers and shareholders.”
 
Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
 
Net sales for the third quarter of 2026 were $2.2 million, unchanged from the prior-year quarter, primarily due to a $0.4 million backorder in the third quarter of 2025. Net sales increased approximately 12% sequentially from the second quarter of 2026. In the third quarter of 2026, the pediatric segment demonstrated continued strength with an increase in net sales of 20% versus the prior-year period.
 
The Company sold eight Aquadex consoles during the third quarter, compared with 12 in the prior-year quarter. Through the first nine months of 2026, the Company sold 32 consoles, compared with 17 during the comparable 2025 period.
 
Gross margin for the third quarter of 2026 was 69.5%, compared with 65.2% in the prior-year quarter. The improvement reflected increased average selling prices and benefits from the Company's transition to contract manufacturing.
 
Operating expenses decreased to $3.6 million from $4.1 million in the prior-year quarter, reflecting continued expense management and operational efficiencies.
 
In the third quarter of 2026, loss from operations improved 24% to $2.1 million, compared with $2.7 million in the prior-year quarter. Net loss was $2.0 million, compared with net income of approximately $0.5 million in the prior-year quarter, which included a significant noncash gain associated with changes in the fair value of warrant liabilities.

During the third quarter of 2026, the Company received $1.4 million in net proceeds from common shares sold under its ATM.

About Aquadex
The Aquadex SmartFlow System is indicated for the continuous ultrafiltration therapy for temporary (up to 8 hours) or extended (longer than 8 hours in patients who require hospitalization) use in adult and pediatric patients weighing 20 kilograms or more whose fluid overload is unresponsive to medical management, including diuretics. All treatments must be administered by a healthcare provider, within an outpatient or inpatient clinical setting, under physician prescription, both of whom having received training in extracorporeal therapies.
 
About Nuwellis
Nuwellis, Inc. (Nasdaq: NUWE) is a medical technology company committed to delivering solutions for patients with cardiorenal conditions. The Company develops solutions designed to support patient care through monitoring, therapy, and data-informed clinical decision-making across acute and chronic care settings. Nuwellis’ portfolio includes commercially available and development-stage technologies addressing complex cardiorenal conditions, with a focus on safety, precision, and scalability across patient populations. For more information, visit www.nuwellis.com.
