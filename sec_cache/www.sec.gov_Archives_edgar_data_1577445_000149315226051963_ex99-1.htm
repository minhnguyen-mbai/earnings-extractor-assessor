Odysight.ai Reports Financial Results for the Nine Months Ended September 30, 2026, and Provides Business Update

 

■ Revenues: $0.9 million for the first nine months, with fourth-quarter revenues expected to increase compared to the third quarter based on current delivery schedules with customers.
   
■ Backlog: $16 million as of September 30, 2026, not yet reflecting a number of fleet-level proposals under negotiation with key customers.
   
■ Cash position: approximately $23.2 million and no financial debt, with approximately $1.2 million collected since quarter end, and runway the Company believes will be sufficient, assuming pipeline conversion, to fund its operations into the second quarter of 2028.
   
■ Commercial milestones: Over the next 12 months, we aim to complete the current phases of our agreements with several key customers, each structured to create a pathway to broader deployments, while advancing the conversion of our fleet-level proposals under negotiation into signed agreements.

 

Ramat Gan, Israel, November 16, 2026 – Odysight.ai Inc. (NASDAQ/TASE: ODYS), a leader in AI-powered visual sensing and predictive maintenance (PdM) solutions for the aerospace, defense, and industrial markets, today announces its financial results for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, and provides a business update.

 

Key Highlights

 

Revenues of $0.9 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026; when focusing on the aerospace domain, up 29% compared to revenue in this domain last year. Based on current delivery schedules agreed with our customers, the Company expects to deliver orders from our existing backlog to five customers in the fourth quarter of 2026 and, as a result, we expect revenues for the fourth quarter of 2026 to increase compared to the third quarter of 2026.

 

Backlog1 of $16 million as of September 30, 2026; our current backlog does not yet reflect a number of fleet-level proposals currently under negotiation with key customers. We believe these opportunities have the potential to substantially grow our backlog in the near term.

 

Cash balance2 of approximately $23.2 million as of September 30, 2026, with no financial debt; in August 2026 we completed a public offering with participation from our chairman, other members of our board of directors and existing shareholders, strengthening our balance sheet as we accelerate our business. We continue to maintain strict cost discipline and see consistent collections from customers, both in Israel and internationally. Since the end of the third quarter, we have collected approximately $1.2 million from some of our key customers.

 

Based on our current cash balance, our existing backlog and our current forecast and operating plan, which assumes the conversion of certain opportunities in our pipeline into signed agreements, we believe our existing resources will be sufficient to fund our operations into the second quarter of 2028. This estimate is subject to a number of assumptions and uncertainties, including the timing and completion of negotiations with customers, the signing of new agreements, the timing of deliveries and collections, and exchange rate fluctuations, any of which could cause our actual cash needs to differ materially; in addition, we could use our available resources sooner than we currently expect.

 

 

 

 

Commercial Highlights

 

  ● Major defense customer (January), two pilot orders – one for monitoring of a critical component within an airborne weapons system, which has been fully delivered, and a second for an operational combat helicopter, which is at an advanced stage of the delivery process.
  ● XP Services U.S. Partnership for UH-60 Black Hawk test flights (March) – completed test flights in June and continued with active flight operations, demonstrating system monitoring capabilities for defined use cases and began presenting the solution to potential U.S. customers.
  ● Naval Air Warfare Center Aircraft Division (NAWCAD) Lakehurst, part of the U.S. Navy (May), Cooperative Research and Development Agreement (CRADA), focusing initially on carrier arresting cables. The project is under delivery. Once completed, it has the potential for expansion into global land-based arresting cables, fixed- and rotary-wing aircraft, ground vehicles, and additional platforms.
  ● Honeywell Aerospace (June), advanced the proof-of-concept collaboration with the APU Division across its Auxiliary Power Units (APU) portfolio, initially focused on the APU air intake. Milestones are under delivery.
  ● Elbit Systems on behalf of the Israeli Ministry of Defense (IMOD) (July), purchase order for integration into several platforms, with delivery milestones underway.
  ● Signed a Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) with Boeing (August) to demonstrate our solutions in laboratory and test environments at two Boeing sites with delivery milestones in progress.
  ● NTA Metropolitan Mass Transit System Ltd. (November), launched a new pilot on the Tel Aviv Light Rail Red Line, with our technology installed to monitor critical railway switches. This is another industrial deployment of our solution in a rail application. Beyond this pilot, we see significant potential for broader deployment across railway networks and infrastructures at large, creating new opportunities for commercial growth while making critical infrastructure safer, more reliable, and more efficient.

Financial highlights for the nine months ended September 30, 2026

 

Revenues were $0.9 million, compared to $2.6 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2025. The decrease in revenues was fully attributable to $1.9 million revenues recognized in the first quarter of 2025 from a Fortune 500 medical customer, of which $1.7 million resulted from the full derecognition of a contract liability, a non-recurring item.

 

Revenues for the first nine months of $0.9 million are primarily attributable to the aerospace domain and are up 29% compared to $0.7 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2025 in the same domain.

 

Backlog1 was $16 million as of September 30, 2026.

 

Cost of Revenues was $0.6 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $1.9 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2025. In the nine months ended September 30, 2025 we had additional costs of approximately $1.4 million related to the medical domain.

 

Cost of revenues for the first nine months of 2026 of $0.6 million is primarily attributable to the aerospace domain, compared to $0.5 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2025 in the same domain.

 

Gross Profit was $0.4 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, compared to gross profit of $0.7 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2025. In the nine months ended September 30, 2025, we had additional profit of approximately $0.5 million related to the medical domain.

 

Gross profit for the first nine months of 2026 of $0.4 million is primarily attributable to the aerospace domain, compared to $0.2 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2025 in the same domain.

 

Gross Margin was approximately 40% for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, compared to approximately 27% for the nine months ended September 30, 2025.

 

Gross margin for the first nine months of 2026 was approximately 40% and is primarily attributable to the aerospace domain, compared to approximately 34% for the nine months ended September 30, 2025 in the same domain.

 

Operating expenses were approximately $14.6 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, compared to approximately $14.4 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2025.

 

The increase was primarily driven by investments in global expansion and sales and marketing activities, as well as costs related to supply chain diversification, and one-time aerospace and cyber certification costs, partially offset by a decrease in stock-based compensation from approximately $2.6 million to approximately $1.8 million. During the period, we implemented efficiency measures, including the adoption of AI tools and refinements to our workforce and subcontractors structure. The resulting reductions were substantially offset by the adverse impact of the USD/NIS exchange rate.

 

Net loss was approximately $13.7 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, compared to approximately $12.8 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2025.

 

Cash Balance2 as of September 30, 2026 was approximately $23.2 million. No financial debt.

 

In August 2026, the Company completed a U.S. underwritten public offering of $11 million in gross proceeds, with participation of our chairman, other members of our board of directors and existing shareholders.

 

About Odysight.ai

 

Odysight.ai, incorporated in Nevada U.S., with European and Israeli subsidiaries, is pioneering the Predictive Maintenance (PdM) and Condition Based Monitoring (CBM) markets with its visualization and AI-powered visual sensing. Providing video sensor-based solutions for critical systems in the aviation, transportation, and energy industries, Odysight.ai leverages proven visual technologies and products from the medical industry. Odysight.ai’s unique video-based sensors, embedded software, and AI algorithms are being deployed in hard-to-reach locations and harsh environments across a variety of PdM and CBM use cases. Odysight.ai’s platform allows maintenance and operations teams visibility into areas that are otherwise inaccessible during normal operation, or where the operating ambience is not suitable for continuous real-time monitoring.
