TPG Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results

San Francisco and Fort Worth, Texas – October 29, 2026 – TPG Inc. (NASDAQ: TPG), a leading global alternative asset management firm, today reported its unaudited results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026. A detailed presentation is available through the Investor Relations section of TPG’s website at shareholders.tpg.com.

"TPG delivered an exceptional third quarter, reflecting broad-based momentum across the firm and continued progress across each of our strategic objectives,” said Jon Winkelried, Chief Executive Officer. “Given the quality of our investment performance and the depth of our relationships, we expect to exceed our fundraising target of $50 billion this year, with $15 billion raised in the quarter and nearly $42 billion year-to-date. With a record $86 billion of dry powder and an increasingly attractive opportunity set ahead, we are well positioned to capitalize on this environment and create long-term value for our stakeholders."

Dividend
TPG has declared a quarterly dividend of $0.55 per share of Class A common stock to holders of record at the close of business on November 9, 2026, payable on November 23, 2026.

About TPG
TPG is a leading global alternative asset management firm, founded in San Francisco in 1992, with $346 billion of assets under management and investment and operational teams around the world. TPG invests across a broadly diversified set of strategies, including private equity, impact, credit, real estate, and market solutions, and our unique strategy is driven by collaboration, innovation, and inclusion. Our teams combine deep product and sector experience with broad capabilities and expertise to develop differentiated insights and add value for our fund investors, portfolio companies, management teams, and communities.
