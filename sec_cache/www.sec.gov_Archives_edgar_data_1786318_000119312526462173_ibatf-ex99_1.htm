International Battery Metals Ltd. Reports Second Quarter of Fiscal Year 2027 Financial Results

Vancouver, British Columbia & Houston, Texas--(BUSINESS WIRE) -- International Battery Metals Ltd. (“IBAT” or the “Company”) (TSXV: IBAT) & (OTCQB: IBATF), an advanced technology provider of modular direct lithium extraction (DLE) systems, today reported financial results for its second quarter of fiscal 2027, ended September 30, 2026.

Don Bender, Interim Chief Executive Officer of IBAT, commented:

“We continue to evaluate opportunities across North America, including through an active laboratory testing program. Our ongoing discussions with North American resource owners have made clear that one of their biggest challenges is they need field data from their own resources before making investment decisions. To meet that need, we have begun engineering a compact pilot plant that can be deployed at sites quickly, and at a lower cost than our existing demonstration plant. We also continue to evaluate opportunities to deploy the demonstration plant. We see the pilot as a more efficient step for resource owners to run IBAT’s DLE technology in the field on live brine and gain data and information for their investment evaluations.

In the Middle East, we participated in a competitive RFQ process for a commercial demonstration program. While we were not selected, encouragingly, we received positive feedback on our technology, our process and our operating costs. We are continuing to evaluate opportunities in the region, which has large plans for lithium development.

In South America, Ensorcia, whose affiliates hold licenses to our technology in Argentina and Chile, continues to evaluate plans for commercial lithium developments. Of note, Ensorcia recently announced in a press release that it signed a binding letter of intent, which is subject to customary closing conditions, to acquire additional resources in Argentina, which could serve as the basis for its initial development program.”

Recent Business Highlights

•
Commercial Deployments in the Americas: IBAT continues to evaluate potential opportunities in North America and South America. Each region and resource is at a different stage of commercial development. IBAT is providing brine testing for prospective customers alongside discussions on deploying the commercial demonstration and a new pilot plant that is in the early stages of development.
•
Pilot plant: IBAT plans to build a small, transportable pilot plant. Multiple North American parties have expressed interest in the pilot as a step towards advancing investment decisions for commercial developments.
•
Middle East: IBAT was not selected in a competitive RFQ process and will continue to evaluate potential opportunities in the region.
•
Bridge financing: IBAT expects to enter into a secured term loan of up to $12.0 million with its largest investor to bridge the Company over approximately the next 12 months. IBAT expects to use up to $4.0 million for the pilot plant, and the balance to fund engineering and general and administrative expenses.

About International Battery Metals Ltd.

International Battery Metals Ltd. is a direct lithium extraction technology company focused on advancing the development of lithium from brine resources. The Company delivers a proprietary DLE process through a modular plant design and architecture built around its media and column systems. Depending on customer requirements, IBAT integrates its DLE process into existing customer flowsheets or specifications or delivers a full flowsheet solution using its proprietary DLE design in conjunction with strategic partnerships. With operational field deployment experience, International Battery Metals is positioned to support the next phase of lithium project development as global battery demand continues to expand. The Company is headquartered in Houston, Texas.
