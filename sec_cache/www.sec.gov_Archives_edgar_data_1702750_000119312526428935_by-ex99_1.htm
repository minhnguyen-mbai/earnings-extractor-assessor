STATEMENTS OF OPERATIONS HIGHLIGHTS

Net Interest Income

Net interest income for the third quarter of 2026 was $103.7 million, an increase of $2.8 million, or 2.8%, from the second quarter of 2026. The increase in net interest income was primarily attributable to higher interest income from investment securities, lower interest expense on other borrowings resulting from reduced wholesale funding levels, and the additional day in the quarter, partially offset by higher interest expense on deposits.

Net interest margin for the third quarter of 2026 was 4.33%, an increase of five basis points compared to the second quarter of 2026. The increase was primarily attributable to higher yields on investment securities and lower Federal Home Loan Bank advances, partially offset by higher rates paid on deposits. Net loan accretion income contributed six basis points to the net interest margin for the quarter, compared to 11 basis points in the prior quarter.

The average cost of total deposits was 1.95% for the third quarter of 2026, an increase of three basis points compared to the second quarter of 2026, primarily due to higher rates paid on interest-bearing accounts.

Provision for Credit Losses

The provision for credit losses was $6.7 million for the third quarter of 2026, a decrease of $414,000, or 5.8%, compared to $7.2 million for the second quarter of 2026. The decrease was primarily attributable to improvements in collateral-related qualitative factors, reflecting favorable trends in certain industry indicators, as well as lower period-end loan and lease balances held for investment. These factors were partially offset by increased reserves associated with certain individually evaluated loans.

Non-interest Income

Non-interest income for the third quarter of 2026 was $17.3 million, an increase of $456,000, or 2.7%, compared to $16.9 million for the second quarter of 2026. The increase was primarily driven by higher bank-owned life insurance income, customer swap activity, and wealth management and trust income, partially offset by lower loan servicing asset revaluation and net gains on sales of loans, as well as less favorable equity securities fair value changes.

Net gains on sales of loans totaled $5.8 million for the quarter, a decrease of $293,000, or 4.8%, compared to the prior quarter. During the third quarter of 2026, we sold $70.8 million of U.S. government guaranteed loans compared to $78.1 million during the second quarter of 2026.

Non-interest Expense

Non-interest expense for the third quarter of 2026 was $59.8 million, an increase of $3.3 million, or 5.9%, compared to $56.5 million for the second quarter of 2026. The increase in non-interest expense was mainly due to a $3.4 million increase in salaries and employee benefits and higher other non-interest expense and legal, audit and other professional fees. These increases were partially offset by lower advertising and promotions expense and lower impairment charges on assets held for sale.

Our efficiency ratio was 48.40%(1) compared to 46.93%(1) for the second quarter of 2026, an increase of 147 basis points. The change in the efficiency ratio was mainly driven by higher non-interest expenses.

Income Taxes

We recorded income tax expense of $12.7 million during the third quarter of 2026, compared to $13.9 million during the second quarter of 2026. The effective tax rates were 23.3% and 25.7% for the third quarter of 2026 and second quarter of 2026, respectively. The decrease was primarily driven by the purchase of renewable energy tax credits during the third quarter.

STATEMENTS OF FINANCIAL CONDITION HIGHLIGHTS

Assets

Total assets were $9.9 billion as of September 30, 2026, a decrease of $33.7 million, or 0.3%, compared to June 30, 2026. The decrease was primarily driven by an $80.9 million decrease in loans and leases held for investment, reflecting loan payoffs and sales that exceeded portfolio growth during the quarter, partially offset by increases in cash and cash equivalents, securities available-for-sale and loans held for sale.

Allowance for Credit Losses

The ACL was $113.0 million as of September 30, 2026, an increase of $1.2 million, or 1.1%, from June 30, 2026. The increase was driven by provision for credit losses on individually assessed loans and net charge-offs on loans and leases. The ACL as a percentage of total loans and leases held for investment increased by three basis points to 1.51%, compared to 1.48% at June 30, 2026.

Net loan and lease charge-offs during the third quarter of 2026 were $5.8 million, or 0.31% of average loans and leases on an annualized basis, an increase of $1.4 million or seven basis points, compared to the second quarter of 2026.

Asset Quality

Non-performing assets were $87.9 million, or 0.89% of total assets, as of September 30, 2026, an increase of $15.6 million, or 21.6%, from June 30, 2026. The increase was primarily attributable to an increase in non-accrual loans and leases. The government guaranteed portion of non-performing loans included in non-performing assets was $9.7 million at September 30, 2026, compared to $8.1 million at June 30, 2026, an increase of $1.5 million, or 18.7%.

Deposits and Other Liabilities

Total deposits increased $168.3 million, up 2.1%, or 8.5% annualized, to $8.0 billion at September 30, 2026 from $7.9 billion as of June 30, 2026. The increase was primarily driven by growth in non-interest-bearing demand deposits, interest-bearing accounts and time deposits, partially offset by lower money market and savings account balances.

Total borrowings and other liabilities were $584.4 million at September 30, 2026, a decrease of $173.1 million, or 22.8%, from $757.5 million at June 30, 2026. The decrease for the quarter was primarily driven by lower Federal Home Loan Bank advances.

Stockholders’ Equity

Total stockholders’ equity was $1.3 billion at September 30, 2026, a decrease of $29.0 million, or 2.2%, from June 30, 2026. The decrease was primarily driven by an increase in accumulated other comprehensive loss related to available-for-sale securities, common share repurchases and dividends declared, partially offset by net income of $41.8 million.

During the quarter ended September 30, 2026, we repurchased 521,980 shares of our common stock.

About Byline Bancorp, Inc.

Headquartered in Chicago, Byline Bancorp, Inc. is the parent company of Byline Bank, a full service commercial bank serving small- and medium-sized businesses, financial sponsors, and consumers. Byline Bank has approximately $9.9 billion in assets and operates 44 branch locations throughout the Chicago and Milwaukee metropolitan areas. Byline Bank offers a broad range of commercial and community banking products and services including small ticket equipment leasing solutions and is one of the top Small Business Administration lenders in the United States.
