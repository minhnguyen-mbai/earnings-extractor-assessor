Winchester Bancorp, Inc.

Announces Results for the Quarter Ended September 30, 2026

Investor Contact

John A. Carroll

President and Chief Executive Officer

IR@WinchesterSavings.com

(781) 729-2130

WINCHESTER, MA, October 28, 2026 - Winchester Bancorp, Inc. (the "Company"), the holding company for Winchester Savings Bank (the "Bank"), today announced its financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, the first quarter of the six-month transition period ending December 31, 2026. The Company reported net income of $1.3 million, or $0.14 per common share, as compared to net income of $1.2 million for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, an increase of $29,000 in net income. Net income for the quarter ended September 30, 2025, was $1.0 million, making the year over year increase $306,000.

“We continue to build on the momentum we established in our first year as a public company,” said John A. Carroll, President and Chief Executive Officer. “Deposit growth continued to lead the way, increasing $53.4 million, or 6.6%, during the quarter, which allowed us to fund net loan growth of $37.9 million, or 4.4%. Total assets grew $44.9 million, or 4.1%, to $1.14 billion. Profitability also continued to improve, with net interest margin expanding to 2.67% from 2.49% and our efficiency ratio improving to 73.1% from 82.8%, both compared to the three months ended September 30, 2025. We remain focused on disciplined growth and on continuing to build value for our shareholders”, Carroll added.

BALANCE SHEET

Total assets were $1.14 billion at September 30, 2026, representing an increase of $45.3 million, or 4.1%, from June 30, 2026.

•
Cash and cash equivalents were $63.2 million, reflecting an increase of $3.1 million, or 5.2%, from June 30, 2026.
•
Net loans were $908.7 million, representing an increase of $37.9 million, or 4.4%, from June 30, 2026, as we continued to experience strong loan demand. The main driver of the new growth was in our construction and residential portfolios, which increased $21.9 million, or 18.8%, and $14.1 million, or 3.5%, respectively, since June 30, 2026.
•
Investment securities totaled $123.2 million, representing a decrease of $1.8 million, or 1.5%, from June 30, 2026, due to matured mortgage backed securities.
•
Deposits totaled $862.7 million, representing an increase of $53.4 million, or 6.6%, since June 30, 2026. The increase in deposits was a result of growth of $33.6 million in municipal customer deposits. As a result of the increase in municipal deposits, money market accounts increased $20.2 million. Certificates of deposit and demand deposit accounts increased $29.8 million and $5.5 million, respectively, while savings accounts decreased $2.1 million.
•
Federal Home Loan Bank borrowings totaled $149.9 million, representing a decrease of $8.2 million, or 5.2%, from $158.2 million at June 30, 2026.
•
Stockholders’ equity was $119.6 million, representing a decrease of $886,000, or 0.7% from $120.5 million at June 30, 2026. The decrease was driven by an increase in comprehensive loss of $2.2 million due to higher unrealized losses on the available for sale portfolio, partially offset by net income of $1.3 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026.

 

 

NET INTEREST INCOME

Net interest income was $7.2 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared to $5.7 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2025, representing an increase of $1.4 million, or 25.2%. Net interest margin expanded by 18 basis points to 2.67% for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 compared to 2.49% for the quarter ended September 30, 2025.

•
The increase in interest income during the quarter ended September 30, 2026, was primarily attributable to the increase in the average balance of loans and investment securities.
•
The increase in interest expense during the year was primarily attributable to higher average interest-bearing deposit balances, partially offset by lower average rates paid on those deposits, lower average borrowings, and reduced borrowing rates.

NON-INTEREST INCOME

Non-interest income was $498,000 for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared to $52,000 for the quarter ended September 30, 2025. Non-interest income for the quarter ended September 30, 2025 includes a one-time loss on the available for sale securities.

NON-INTEREST EXPENSE

Non-interest expense was $5.6 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, representing an increase of $821,000, or 17.1%, from the quarter ended September 30, 2025 due to increases in salaries and employee benefits, marketing and data processing expense and general and administrative expenses.

ASSET QUALITY

Asset quality remains strong. The allowance for credit losses on loans in total and as a percentage of total gross loans as of September 30, 2026 was $5.1 million and 0.56%, compared to $4.4 million and 0.55% as of September 30, 2025.

•
During the quarter ended September 30, 2026, the Company recorded no charge offs compared to net charge offs of $270,000 for the quarter ended September 30, 2025.
•
Non-performing assets totaled $1.9 million, or 0.17% of total assets, as of September 30, 2026, compared to $1.9 million, or 0.19% of total assets, as of September 30, 2025.

ABOUT WINCHESTER BANCORP, INC.

Winchester Bancorp, Inc. is the mid-tier holding company of Winchester Savings Bank and is the majority owned subsidiary of Winchester Bancorp, MHC. Winchester Savings Bank's mission is to operate and grow a profitable community-oriented financial institution that is dedicated to meeting the banking needs of individuals and small businesses in the communities in which it operates.
