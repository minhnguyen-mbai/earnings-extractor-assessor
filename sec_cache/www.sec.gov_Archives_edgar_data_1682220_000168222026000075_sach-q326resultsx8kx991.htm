SACHEM CAPITAL REPORTS
THIRD QUARTER 2026 RESULTS

– Provides Update on Pending Asset Contribution Transaction with Industrial Realty Group (IRG) –
BRANFORD, Conn., November [ ], 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Sachem Capital Corp. (NYSE American: SACH) (“Sachem” or the “Company”), a real estate lender specializing in originating, underwriting, funding, servicing, and managing a portfolio of loans secured by first mortgages on real property, today announced its financial results for the quarter and nine months ended September 30, 2026 and provided an update on the pending asset contribution transaction (the “Transaction”) with Industrial Realty Group (“IRG”).

John Villano, CPA, Sachem’s Chief Executive Officer, commented, “During the third quarter, we continued to make meaningful progress repositioning our loan portfolio, substantially reducing non-accrual loans since year-end. We also ended the quarter with a solid cash position. Our announced transaction with IRG, which would combine Sachem with a scaled portfolio of industrial real estate, represents a compelling opportunity for our shareholders. We are working diligently toward completion, including preparing pro forma financial statements and proxy materials. While the transaction remains subject to shareholder approval and other customary closing conditions, we remain focused on bringing it to a close, as we believe it can create significant long-term value for our shareholders.”

Contribution Agreement with Industrial Realty Group Global, LLC

As previously announced, the Company and IRG, a private real estate development and investment firm specializing in the acquisition, development and management of commercial and industrial real estate throughout the United States, entered into a definitive contribution agreement on May 17, 2026, under which IRG will contribute 98 industrial assets (“the Contribution Portfolio”) from its 200-asset portfolio owned by IRG and/or its partners to Sachem, and once completed, the combined company will operate as IRG Realty Trust, Inc. ("IRGT").

IRG will contribute the Contribution Portfolio to a newly formed Sachem operating partnership in exchange for operating partnership units and Class B common stock, based on a deemed value of $2.00 per Sachem common share. Following the closing, IRG is expected to hold approximately 94.1% of the operating partnership units, with Sachem shareholders retaining approximately 5.9%. At closing, Sachem expects to effect a 20-to-1 reverse stock split and change its name to IRG Realty Trust, Inc. The transaction was unanimously approved by Sachem’s Board of Directors and remains subject to shareholder approval and other closing conditions.

Upon closing, IRGT is expected to own 98 industrial properties having a gross real estate asset value of $2.9 billion, plus Sachem's approximately $467 million of total assets in direct and indirect mortgage loans, investments in developmental and owned real estate, and other assets.

Sachem’s Results of Operations for the Quarter Ended September 30, 2026

Net interest income was $1.1 million compared to $2.7 million in the third quarter of 2025. The year-over-year change was primarily due to a $1.7 million reduction in interest income from loans as average performing loan balances were lower by $36.3 million and the effective yield on performing loans declined. As the Company continued to reduce its limited liability company investments, interest income from limited liability company investments decreased $0.4 million while interest expense decreased $0.5 million due to lower average borrowings. Utilizing the average performing loans held for investment balance for the quarter of $231.8 million, the effective yield on performing loans was 11.5%. Comparatively, using the average performing loans held for investment balance for the third quarter of 2025 of $268.1 million, the effective yield on performing loans was 12.4%.

The Company’s net interest margin was 1.4% for the third quarter, compared to 2.9% for the third quarter 2025. Net interest margin represents net interest income, calculated as annualized interest income less annualized interest expense, expressed as a percentage of average loans held for investment outstanding for the applicable period. The change in net interest margin reflects both structural and cyclical factors. Structurally, refinancing activity during 2025 increased the weighted average cost of capital. Cyclically, lower average earning assets reduced interest-earning balances.

Provision for credit losses related to loans held for investment was $0.5 million, compared with $0.8 million in the third quarter of 2025. The decrease was primarily due to stabilization in collateral valuations for loans previously reserved and fewer nonperforming loans.

Total other income was $1.8 million compared to $4.1 million in the same quarter last year, primarily due to decline in fee income on loans due to lower new loan origination volume, the absence of $1.4 million of gains on equity securities recorded in the same quarter last year, and decrease in rental income as a result of the sale of developmental real estate in December 2025.

Total operating costs and expenses for the third quarter of 2026 were $4.6 million compared to $5.0 million in the same quarter last year. The decrease was primarily related to lower compensation and employee benefits and general and administrative expenses, partially offset by transaction expenses, as discussed further below.

•Compensation and employee benefits were $1.9 million, compared to $2.3 million in the same quarter last year, reflecting nonrecurring awards in the same quarter last year.
•General and administrative expenses were $1.4 million, versus $1.7 million from the same quarter last year, primarily due to lower costs associated with our investments in developmental real estate.
•Transaction expenses were $0.8 million versus none in the same quarter last year, all of which were incurred in connection with the Transaction. We expect to incur additional material costs prior to the closing of the Transaction.

Balance Sheet

At quarter end, total assets were $467.4 million compared to $460.0 million as of December 31, 2025, primarily reflecting a $30.5 million increase in cash and a $40.9 million increase in investments in developmental real estate, partially offset by a $57.2 million decrease in loans held for investment, net. About Sachem Capital Corp

Sachem is a mortgage REIT that specializes in originating, underwriting, funding, servicing, and managing a portfolio of loans secured by first mortgages on real property. It offers short-term (i.e., one to three years), secured, nonbanking loans to real estate investors to fund their acquisition, renovation, development, rehabilitation, or improvement of properties. The Company’s primary underwriting criteria is a conservative loan to value ratio. The properties securing the loans are generally classified as residential or commercial real estate and, typically, are held for resale or investment. Loans are secured by mortgage liens on real estate and often are personally guaranteed by the principal(s) of the borrower. The Company also makes opportunistic real estate purchases apart from its lending activities.
