Oil States International, Inc. reported net income of $3.1 million, or $0.05 per share, and Adjusted EBITDA of $20.1 million for the third quarter of 2026 on revenues of $169.8 million. These results compare to revenues of $156.7 million, net income of $5.9 million, or $0.10 per share, and Adjusted EBITDA of $19.0 million reported in the second quarter of 2026.
Oil States’ President and Chief Executive Officer, Lloyd Hajdik, stated:
“Our third quarter results exceeded the guidance we provided back in July and reflect continued execution across our diversified portfolio and the resilience of our offshore and international operations. Revenues increased 8% sequentially, driven by strong demand within our Offshore Manufactured Products and Downhole Technologies segments. We generated Adjusted EBITDA of $20.1 million. Contract bookings in our Offshore Manufactured Products segment increased 18% sequentially to $135 million, resulting in a book-to-bill ratio of 1.3x and backlog reaching its highest level since December 2014.
“Looking ahead, we remain focused on expanding our backlog, delivering profitable growth and strong cash flows, and returning capital to stockholders through disciplined capital allocation. Backed by our global manufacturing, engineering and service capabilities, strong financial position and ongoing strategic initiatives, we believe Oil States is well positioned to benefit from a constructive multi-year offshore and international investment cycle. Our innovative, technologically advanced product and service offerings, combined with disciplined execution and selective growth investments, provide a strong foundation for sustainable long-term stockholder value creation.”
Business Segment Results
(See Segment Data and Adjusted Segment EBITDA tables below)
Offshore Manufactured Products
Offshore Manufactured Products reported revenues of $104.4 million, operating income of $14.8 million and Adjusted Segment EBITDA of $18.8 million in the third quarter of 2026, compared to revenues of $92.7 million, operating income of $13.9 million and Adjusted Segment EBITDA of $17.9 million reported in the second quarter of 2026. Adjusted Segment EBITDA margin was 18% in the third quarter of 2026, compared to 19% in the second quarter of 2026.
Backlog totaled $482 million as of September 30, 2026, its highest level since December 2014. Third quarter bookings totaled $135 million, yielding a quarterly book-to-bill ratio of 1.3x and raising the year-to-date ratio to 1.2x.
Completion and Production Services
Completion and Production Services reported revenues of $23.2 million, operating income of $3.5 million and Adjusted Segment EBITDA of $6.1 million in the third quarter of 2026, compared to revenues of $24.3 million, operating income of $3.9 million and Adjusted Segment EBITDA of $6.6 million reported in the second quarter of 2026. Adjusted Segment EBITDA margin was 26% in the third quarter of 2026, compared to 27% in the second quarter of 2026.
Downhole Technologies
Downhole Technologies reported revenues of $42.2 million, operating income of $2.1 million and Adjusted Segment EBITDA of $3.6 million in the third quarter of 2026, compared to revenues of $39.7 million, operating income of $2.7 million and Adjusted Segment EBITDA of $4.2 million in the second quarter of 2026. Adjusted Segment EBITDA margin was 8% in the third quarter of 2026, compared to 11% in the second quarter of 2026.
Corporate
Corporate reported an operating loss of $14.1 million and an Adjusted Segment EBITDA loss of $8.3 million in the third quarter of 2026, compared to an operating loss of $8.9 million and an Adjusted Segment EBITDA loss of $9.7 million in the second quarter of 2026.
Operating loss in the third quarter of 2026 included charges of $5.5 million associated primarily with the retirement of the Company’s former President and Chief Executive Officer and facility exit costs.
Interest Expense, Net
Net interest expense totaled $1.3 million in the third quarter of 2026, which included $0.6 million of non-cash amortization of deferred debt issuance costs – the majority of which was associated with the July 2026 expiration of $30 million in undrawn lender commitments under the Company’s term loan facility.


Income Taxes
During the third quarter of 2026, the Company recognized income tax expense of $2.1 million, which included the impact of changes in valuation allowances recorded against deferred tax assets, certain discrete tax items and other non-deductible expenses, on pre-tax income of $5.3 million.
Cash Flows
During the third quarter of 2026, the Company generated $5.3 million of cash flows in operations, tempered by net working capital investments of $9.6 million. Proceeds from the sale of assets totaled $5.1 million during the quarter, which were partially offset by $3.2 million in capital expenditures.
The Company purchased $4.0 million of its common stock in the third quarter of 2026 under a Board-approved program that expired on October 24, 2026. On October 29, 2026, the Company's Board of Directors approved a new $50.0 million stock repurchase plan, which extends through October 2028.
Financial Condition
At September 30, 2026, the Company had cash on-hand of $22.9 million. Borrowings and letters of credit outstanding under the Cash Flow Credit Agreement totaled $19.8 million and $17.0 million, respectively, leaving $58.0 million available to be drawn.

About Oil States
Oil States International, Inc. is a global provider of manufactured products and services to customers in the energy, military and industrial sectors. The Company’s manufactured products include highly engineered capital equipment and consumable products. Oil States is headquartered in Houston, Texas with manufacturing and service facilities strategically located across the globe. Oil States is publicly traded on the New York Stock Exchange and NYSE Texas under the symbol “OIS”.
For more information on the Company, please visit Oil States International’s website at www.oilstatesintl.com.
