Myriad Genetics Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results;
Raises Mid-Point of Full-Year 2026 Revenue Guidance Following Solid Execution
Definitive Agreement to Sell Prenatal Health Business to Support Strategic Growth Initiatives

Highlights
•Third quarter 2026 revenue was $202.7 million, representing a 6% sequential increase and a 1% decrease year-over-year. Higher Cancer Care Continuum (CCC) product mix and continued progress on payer engagement and revenue cycle management initiatives drove total average revenue per test increases of 7% sequentially and 1% year-over-year, partially offset by total test volume declines of 1% and 2%, respectively.
•On October 19, 2026, entered into a definitive agreement to sell the Prenatal Health business to Labcorp for $210 million in cash, plus the value of related inventory at closing. Upon close, the sale is expected to be accretive to revenue growth and profitability, while strengthening the balance sheet.
•Raises mid-point of full-year 2026 revenue guidance to reflect third quarter 2026 results and current business trends for the remainder of 2026.

SALT LAKE CITY, November 4, 2026 – Myriad Genetics, Inc. (NASDAQ: MYGN), a leader in molecular diagnostic testing and precision medicine, today announced financial results for its third quarter ended September 30, 2026, and raised the mid-point of revenue guidance for the full-year 2026.

1

“We have made meaningful progress on our path to accelerate profitable growth and strengthen the company’s financial profile. Our third quarter 2026 financial results exceeded our expectations, led by our on-going focus on the Cancer Care Continuum (CCC).  Average revenue per CCC test in the third quarter 2026 was higher than in the second quarter 2026 and reflects our team's ongoing work and early progress with payer engagement and revenue cycle management initiatives," said Sam Raha, President and CEO, Myriad Genetics. “The sale of our Prenatal Health business is a decisive step to support our strategic intent to increase profitable growth by focusing on the Cancer Care Continuum including screening, treatment selection and monitoring along with remission surveillance. We expect this action to benefit healthcare providers and patients we serve, as well as our employees and shareholders, and improve Myriad's revenue growth rate, profitability, and liquidity going forward.  Lastly, we are increasing the mid-point of our full-year 2026 revenue guidance modestly, as we balance the positive third quarter 2026 performance with the timing of the sale of the Prenatal Health business, which is expected to close by early 2027."

Financial and Operational Highlights
•Third quarter 2026 test volume for Cancer Care Continuum and Mental Health increased 3% year-over-year to 239,000 tests, while Prenatal Health test volume decreased 11% to 138,000 tests, reflecting ongoing trends in the business, which the Company recently announced it plans to divest.
•Third quarter of 2026 average revenue per test increased 1% year-over-year reflecting product mix and incremental progress with payer engagement and revenue cycle management initiatives.

Cash Flow and Liquidity
Third quarter 2026 cash flow used in operations was $6.5 million and adjusted operating cash flow in the third quarter of 2026 was $3.7 million. Capital expenditures and capitalization of internal-use software costs totaled $2.1 million in the third quarter of 2026, resulting in adjusted free cash flow of $1.6 million in the third quarter of 2026.

As of the end of the third quarter of 2026, the company had cash and cash equivalents of $107.1 million.



Business Performance and Highlights
Cancer Care Continuum
The Cancer Care Continuum business delivered revenue of $123.9 million in the third quarter of 2026.
•Third quarter 2026 hereditary cancer testing revenue decreased 1% year-over-year due to decreases in reimbursement levels, partially offset by a 6% year-over-year increase in volume. Average revenue per hereditary cancer test in the third quarter 2026 improved 9% over the second quarter 2026, reflecting early progress with revenue cycle initiatives and other programs.
•In late October 2026, new MONSTAR-SCREEN-3 data was presented as part of the European Society for Medical Oncology Congress 2026. We believe this data reinforces the importance of highly sensitive molecular residual disease (MRD) testing after curative-intent surgery in patients with resectable colorectal and pancreatic cancers, where earlier insight into recurrence risk may help inform monitoring and treatment decisions.
•The company continues to engage the Molecular Diagnostics Program, administered by Palmetto GBA, a Medicare Administrative Contractor for the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services, for a Local Coverage Determination to establish Medicare coverage of its Precise MRD test for breast cancer patients.
•In October 2026, Myriad Genetics announced a collaboration with SOPHia GENETICS to advance new prostate cancer-specific genomic instability score (PrGIS) technology to support precision oncology approaches with AstraZeneca. The collaboration aims to advance PrGIS in clinical trials and expand access to precision oncology medicines through potential companion diagnostic solutions.
•In October 2026, Dr. Luis Diaz Jr. and team at Memorial Sloan Kettering Cancer Center published findings in the American Association for Cancer Research's Cancer Discovery Journal that further support broad-based germline testing beyond conventional age-based criteria, reflecting the prevalence of hereditary risk even among late-onset cancer cases1.

Mental Health
GeneSight test revenue was $39.5 million in the third quarter of 2026.
•Third quarter 2026 revenue increased 2% year-over-year reflecting 3% year-over-year GeneSight volume growth, partially offset by a 1% year-over-year decline in average revenue per test, which reflects general market dynamics.

Prenatal Health
The Prenatal Health business delivered revenue of $39.3 million in the third quarter of 2026.
•Prenatal testing revenue in the third quarter 2026 declined 12% year-over-year, as volume decreased 11% year-over-year.
•On October 19, 2026, the company entered into a definitive agreement to sell its Prenatal Health testing business, which includes the Prequel, Foresight, SneakPeek, and FirstGene tests to Labcorp for $210 million in cash, plus the value of the inventory of the Prenatal Health business at closing. The transaction is subject to customary closing conditions, including regulatory approvals and is expected to close by early 2027.

About Myriad Genetics
Myriad Genetics is a leading molecular diagnostic and precision medicine company committed to advancing health and well-being for all. Myriad Genetics develops and commercializes molecular tests that help patients and providers uncover genetic insights. Our tests assess the risk of developing disease or disease progression and guide treatment decisions across medical specialties where molecular insights can significantly improve patient care, support earlier detection, enable more precise treatment and contribute to lowering healthcare costs.
